# Prometheus IQ Executive Briefing Packet

Prepared for: Chris and Dallas Leadership  
Company: Prometheus  
Product: Prometheus IQ  
Purpose: Private pilot discussion for field sales / LSM performance improvement

## 1. Executive Summary

Prometheus IQ is a field-sales intelligence platform designed to help outside sales teams know where to go, verify activity, learn from results, and improve performance over time.

For Fitness Connection, the opportunity is to test whether a focused field-sales tool can improve LSM productivity, reduce wasted field time, increase visibility, and help sales teams generate more measurable results from outside marketing.

The proposed next step is a controlled private pilot with approved users, limited access, clear success metrics, and no disruption to current systems.

## 2. Why Now

Fitness Connection already invests time and energy into LSM activity. The opportunity is to make that activity easier to measure, verify, and improve.

The sales environment is also changing. Digital tools are everywhere, but face-to-face field activity remains valuable when it is focused, consistent, and measurable.

Prometheus IQ gives leadership a way to test whether outside activity can become more structured without making the rep's job harder.

## 3. The Business Problem

Field sales and LSM activity are valuable, but they are often difficult to manage consistently.

Common challenges include:

- Reps spend time deciding where to go instead of taking action.
- Field visits are not always easy to verify.
- Managers may not have clear visibility into what happened in the field.
- Follow-up opportunities can be lost after a conversation.
- Good locations, time windows, and visit patterns are not always captured in a usable way.

The result is that valuable field activity can become hard to measure, repeat, and improve.

## 4. The Prometheus IQ Solution

Prometheus IQ gives field sales teams a simple operating layer for outside sales activity.

At a high level, the platform helps a rep:

- See a focused field plan.
- Visit real locations.
- Log conversations, leads, visits, and results.
- Attach GPS-verified visit context when appropriate.
- Track follow-up opportunities.
- Give managers a clearer view of field activity.

Prometheus IQ helps field teams turn outside activity into measurable intelligence.

## 5. GPS Verification And Route Intelligence

Prometheus IQ supports GPS pin-drop verification for business visits. This is designed to confirm that a rep was physically near a logged visit location at the time of entry. It is not designed as continuous employee tracking.

Prometheus IQ also helps reps focus on practical field decisions:

- Where should the rep go next?
- Which locations deserve attention?
- What outcomes are being produced from field activity?
- What follow-up opportunities should not be missed?

The pilot should evaluate outcomes, not internal technology.

## 6. Proposed Pilot Structure

Recommended pilot setup:

- 3 approved users.
- 1 approved club or market area.
- 14 days of testing.
- 1 weekly review during the pilot.
- 1 final private results report after completion.

User types:

- Sales rep / consultant: logs visits, conversations, leads, outcomes, and follow-ups.
- Manager / reviewer: reviews activity summaries, route performance, and measurable outcomes.

Pilot rules:

- Approved testers only.
- Approved locations and business categories.
- No sensitive company data entered unless approved.
- GPS verification only during intentional visit logging.
- Results reviewed weekly.

## 7. Expected Benefits

If the pilot is successful, Fitness Connection could gain:

- Better visibility into LSM activity.
- More consistent field execution.
- Less wasted time deciding where to go.
- Improved accountability through GPS-verified visits.
- Better follow-up discipline.
- More useful performance reporting from field activity.
- A clearer way to identify which locations, categories, and time windows produce results.

## 8. Pilot Success Metrics

The pilot should be judged on simple, measurable outcomes:

- Locations visited.
- GPS-verified visits.
- LSM events attended.
- Conversations logged.
- Leads generated.
- Membership units sold.
- Follow-ups completed.
- Time spent in approved field activity.
- Productivity per field session.
- Rep adoption and ease of use.
- Manager visibility.

The pilot should be considered successful if it shows clear evidence in at least three areas:

- Approved users consistently log field activity.
- GPS-verified visits improve accountability and clarity.
- Managers gain better visibility into LSM execution.
- The tool feels fast enough for real field use.
- Field activity produces measurable leads, units, follow-ups, or productivity improvement.

## 9. Recommended Next Step

The recommended next step is a controlled private review followed by a limited pilot.

Suggested process:

1. Leadership reviews the concept at a high level.
2. Prometheus provides a private demonstration under appropriate confidentiality expectations.
3. Approved users are selected.
4. Pilot scope, duration, and success metrics are confirmed.
5. Pilot runs for 14 days.
6. Results are summarized and reviewed with leadership.

Prometheus IQ is built for a simple goal:

Help field sales teams know where to go, verify what happened, and improve from every visit.
