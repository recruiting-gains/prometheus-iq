# Prometheus IQ Pilot Approval Cover Sheet

Audience: Chris, Dallas Leadership, COO, President, Operations Leadership  
Purpose: Controlled private pilot approval discussion

## 1. What Is Prometheus IQ?

Prometheus IQ is a field-sales intelligence tool designed to help outside sales teams know where to go, verify field activity, log outcomes, and improve performance from real-world visits.

It supports the field activity that happens before and during LSM outreach.

## 2. What Is Being Requested?

Approval to run a small, controlled 14-day private pilot.

The purpose is to determine whether Prometheus IQ can improve LSM visibility, accountability, route usefulness, and measurable field-sales outcomes.

## 3. Who Participates?

- 3 approved users.
- 1 approved club or market area.
- Approved management / reviewer access only.
- Weekly review during the pilot.
- Final private report after the pilot.

## 4. What Data Is Collected?

The pilot would track basic field-sales activity and outcomes:

- Locations visited.
- GPS-verified visit logs.
- LSM events attended.
- Conversations or leads generated.
- Membership units sold.
- Follow-ups completed.
- Time spent in approved field activity.
- Productivity per field session.

## 5. What Safeguards Exist?

- Approved users only.
- User consent before participation.
- GPS verification only when a user intentionally logs a visit.
- No continuous location tracking.
- No sensitive company data entered unless approved.
- Management visibility limited to authorized pilot activity.
- No public use of Fitness Connection name, data, results, employee information, or testimonials without written approval.
- Pilot can be stopped if HR, legal, privacy, security, or operational concerns arise.

## 6. What Counts As Success?

The pilot is successful if it provides clear evidence that Prometheus IQ improves at least three of the following:

- Consistent field activity logging.
- GPS-verified visit accountability.
- Manager visibility into LSM execution.
- Rep ease of use in the field.
- Leads, units, follow-ups, or productivity per field session.

The pilot does not need to prove company-wide rollout. It only needs to show whether Prometheus IQ is worth a larger controlled evaluation.

## 7. Why Should Leadership Consider This Pilot?

Fitness Connection already invests time and effort into LSM activity. Prometheus IQ offers a controlled way to test whether that activity can become more focused, measurable, and accountable without disrupting existing systems.

Recommended decision:

Approve a limited 14-day private pilot with 3 approved users, user consent, weekly review, and a final private results report.
