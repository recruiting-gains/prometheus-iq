# Enterprise Sales Methodology

Purpose: define how Prometheus sells across industries without being limited to the gym validation environment.

## Category Definition

Prometheus Route IQ is the field-sales operating layer that sits before and during CRM activity.

CRM systems record contacts, opportunities, accounts, and pipeline after sales activity has created something worth tracking. Prometheus helps outside sales teams decide where to go, verify field activity, capture visit outcomes, learn which locations produce, and improve tomorrow's route.

## Core Sales Thesis

Outside sales teams lose revenue because field work is often managed through habit, memory, rep preference, and incomplete reporting. Prometheus converts field activity into an operating system:

- Reps know where to go.
- Managers know what happened.
- Teams learn which territories, location types, and time windows produce.
- Executives can evaluate outside-sales productivity with evidence.

## ICP

Best-fit customers have:

- Outside reps, canvassers, field marketers, appointment setters, territory managers, or local outreach teams.
- Daily or weekly field activity that is hard to observe from the office.
- Meaningful travel time, fuel cost, or territory inefficiency.
- A measurable output such as units, appointments, inspections, demos, consultations, proposals, claims, enrollments, renewals, or qualified leads.
- Managers who care about both activity quality and outcome quality.
- A clear route from pilot results to paid deployment.

Poor-fit customers have:

- No outside sales motion.
- No measurable field outcome.
- No manager accountable for adoption.
- No willingness to run a controlled pilot.
- A demand for CRM replacement before Prometheus has proven field-sales value.

## Sales Process

### 1. Market Hypothesis

Before outreach, define:

- Industry.
- Field role.
- Primary field outcome.
- Cost of wasted field activity.
- Existing tool gap.
- Likely economic buyer.
- Likely pilot sponsor.

### 2. Discovery

Use discovery to determine whether Prometheus can create measurable field-sales improvement. Do not demo before confirming the customer has a real field execution problem.

### 3. Qualification

Qualify for pain, measurable outcome, pilot authority, data access, operational sponsor, and deployment path. A friendly prospect without pilot ownership is not a qualified opportunity.

### 4. Value Hypothesis

Translate discovery into one concise business case:

`If Prometheus helps [team] improve [field behavior] by [target amount], the customer gains [measurable business outcome].`

### 5. Demo

Demo the operating workflow, not the feature list. Show how a rep plans a day, logs field activity, and how management learns from the results.

### 6. Pilot

Run a controlled pilot with written scope, users, success metrics, review cadence, privacy boundaries, and decision criteria.

### 7. Close

The close should convert proven pilot outcomes into a paid implementation plan, not pressure the buyer into a vague subscription.

### 8. Onboard

Onboarding should create operating adoption: rep usage, manager review rhythm, executive reporting, and support channel.

### 9. Expand

Expansion follows proof. Do not expand before the first deployment has adoption, measurable value, and an accountable owner.

### 10. Renew

Renewal is earned through usage, outcome evidence, manager dependency, and executive confidence.

Economic buyers:

- Owner
- COO
- VP Sales
- VP Field Operations
- Regional Director
- General Manager

Technical and risk reviewers:

- IT
- Security
- Legal
- HR
- Operations
- Data owner

Daily users:

- Outside sales reps
- Canvassers
- Field marketers
- Territory managers
- Appointment setters

Internal champions:

- Manager whose team has route waste or visibility gaps.
- Rep leader responsible for daily production.
- Operator who needs better field execution evidence.

## Sales Motion By Company Stage

Small business:

- Direct founder conversation.
- Short discovery call.
- One-team pilot.
- Owner reviews outcomes.
- Close on practical value and simple pricing.

Regional company:

- Manager and executive sponsor required.
- Multi-rep pilot.
- Weekly operating review.
- Close on expansion to region or market.

National company:

- Executive sponsor, regional pilot owner, IT/security review.
- Cross-market pilot design.
- Standardized onboarding.
- Close on phased rollout.

Enterprise:

- Formal buying committee.
- Security, legal, procurement, data governance.
- Implementation plan by role and region.
- Executive business review before renewal and expansion.

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not sell Prometheus as a CRM replacement.
- Do not imply live production integrations exist before they are built.
- Do not promise industry-specific product behavior without confirming product support.
- Do not use Fitness Connection evidence as public proof without approval.
- Do not overstate pilot results.
- Do not skip written success criteria.
