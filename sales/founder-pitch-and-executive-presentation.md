# Founder Pitch And Executive Presentation

Purpose: standardize how Prometheus is explained by the founder and in executive rooms.

## Founder Pitch

Short pitch:

Prometheus IQ is an AI operating system for outside sales teams. It helps reps know where to go, verify what happened, learn which field activity produces, and improve the next route. Most sales tools start after a lead exists. Prometheus focuses on the field work that creates the lead in the first place.

Problem:

Outside sales is still too dependent on guessing, memory, inconsistent reporting, and manager follow-up. Reps waste time and fuel. Managers cannot always see what happened in the field. Companies struggle to learn which locations, categories, territories, and time windows actually produce.

Solution:

Prometheus gives teams a repeatable field-sales workflow: plan the route, execute visits, log outcomes, review what produced, and improve the next route from real data.

Why now:

Organizations already invest heavily in CRM, analytics, and sales enablement, but field activity before CRM creation remains under-instrumented. AI can now help turn visit-level data into better daily decisions.

Initial proof:

The first production validation environment is gym membership field sales. That environment proves the field workflow under real outside-sales pressure. The product category is broader than gyms.

Commercial path:

Start with controlled pilots, prove measurable field execution improvement, convert to paid deployments, then expand by team, market, region, and enterprise.

Title:

`Prometheus IQ: The Field-Sales Operating System`

Core message:

Prometheus helps outside sales teams decide where to go, verify what happened, learn what produces, and improve the next route.

Message:

Outside sales activity is high-value but often poorly measured before a lead reaches CRM.

Evidence to include:

- Current customer field motion.
- Cost of wasted rep time.
- Visibility gaps.
- Existing tool limitations.

Message:

CRM and route tools are useful but incomplete.

Contrast:

- CRM: records contacts and opportunities.

- Prometheus: improves field-sales execution and learning.

Plan, visit, verify, log, learn, improve.

Message:

Prometheus should be evaluated with clear security, privacy, HR, legal, and operational boundaries.

- Approved data.
- Access control.

If the pilot proves value, the paid deployment should be tied to measured field-sales improvement.

Include:

- Pricing model.
- Deployment scope.

Message:

Ask for a specific next decision.

Options:

- Approve pilot.
- Approve deeper discovery.
- Route to required reviewer.
- Decline with reason.

## Executive Talk Track

Opening:

`We are not asking you to replace your CRM or launch a company-wide rollout. The question is whether a focused field-sales operating layer can help your reps spend time in better places, give managers clearer evidence, and create measurable outcomes from outside sales activity.`

Pilot close:

`The cleanest way to evaluate this is a controlled pilot with approved users, defined data boundaries, weekly review, and a final decision based on evidence.`

Paid conversion close:

`The pilot evidence shows whether Prometheus should become part of the operating rhythm. If the results justify it, the next step is a paid deployment with clear onboarding, reporting, and renewal metrics.`

## Presentation Rules

- Lead with customer problem, not product history.
- Use the gym environment as validation, not market limitation.
- Separate verified evidence from assumptions.
- Keep proprietary logic out of executive decks.
- Do not use customer names, results, or screenshots externally without approval.
- Every deck should end with a decision, owner, and date.
