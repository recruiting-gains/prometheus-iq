# Enterprise Industry Playbooks

Purpose: define how Prometheus IQ should be sold into enterprise outside-sales markets.

Prometheus IQ is the AI operating system for organizations that sell in the physical world. Gym sales is the first validation environment only. These playbooks translate the same core operating loop into industry-specific sales motions:

`decide where to go -> verify what happened -> log outcomes -> learn what produces -> improve the next route`

Use these playbooks for discovery, outbound targeting, demo planning, pilot design, executive business cases, expansion planning, and renewal preparation.

## Universal Sales Position

Prometheus should be sold as the field-sales operating layer before and during CRM activity.

Core value:

- Better field direction.
- Less wasted travel and rep time.
- More visible field activity.
- Better manager coaching.
- Compounding territory and visit intelligence.
- Clearer executive reporting on outside-sales productivity.

Universal sales boundary:

- Do not sell Prometheus as a CRM replacement.
- Do not claim unsupported integrations.
- Do not promise surveillance or background tracking.
- Do not expose proprietary scoring logic.
- Do not overstate industry-specific compliance readiness before review.

## Healthcare

### Customer Profile

Healthcare organizations with field outreach teams: clinics, specialty practices, home health, physical therapy groups, imaging centers, behavioral health providers, senior care networks, and provider groups that depend on referral-source development.

Best-fit teams have territory reps visiting physicians, community partners, discharge planners, employer groups, senior centers, and local healthcare organizations.

### Pain Points

- Referral-source activity is hard to see and compare across territories.
- Reps rely on memory, spreadsheets, and recurring visits that may not produce.
- Leadership cannot easily tell which sources produce qualified referrals.
- Field notes may be inconsistent or too sensitive for casual systems.
- High-value referral relationships are slow to build and easy to neglect.

### Sales Process

Lead with referral-source intelligence, not generic route planning. Discovery should map referral sources, visit cadence, relationship stage, referral output, and compliance boundaries. Demo the rep workflow around approved referral-source visits and the manager workflow around activity quality and source performance.

### Decision Makers

- CEO, COO, or practice owner.
- VP business development.
- Director of physician relations.
- Regional director.
- Compliance or privacy reviewer.
- IT/security reviewer for larger organizations.

### Buying Triggers

- Referral volume is flat or declining.
- New market, clinic, location, or service line launch.
- Field team expansion.
- Leadership lacks visibility into rep activity.
- Competitors are winning referral relationships.
- Manual referral-source reporting is unreliable.

### Prometheus IQ Value Proposition

Prometheus helps healthcare field teams focus on the right referral sources, track approved outreach activity, identify productive relationship patterns, and improve territory coverage without becoming a surveillance tool.

### Pilot Strategy

Pilot with 2 to 5 reps in one market or service line for 30 days. Use only approved non-patient field activity data. Measure referral-source visits, follow-ups, relationship-stage movement, qualified referral conversations, and manager review quality.

### Enterprise Rollout Strategy

Expand by service line, region, or referral territory after the pilot proves adoption and leadership value. Standardize compliant note rules, approved activity types, manager review cadence, and referral-source categories before broad rollout.

### ROI Discussion

Discuss value through improved referral-source coverage, higher-quality outreach, better follow-up discipline, and earlier identification of productive sources. Use customer-provided referral economics; do not estimate patient value without customer approval.

### Objection Handling

- "We have compliance concerns." Response: start with approved outreach activity only and define what data cannot be entered.
- "Our reps already know their doctors." Response: Prometheus preserves rep judgment and makes relationship coverage visible enough to coach and scale.
- "We use CRM." Response: CRM records account history; Prometheus improves daily field direction and visit-level learning.

### Success Metrics

- Active rep adoption.
- Referral-source visits logged.
- Follow-ups completed.
- Productive source categories identified.
- Relationship-stage movement.
- Qualified referral conversations.
- Manager review cadence.
- Compliance-safe usage.

## B2B SaaS

### Customer Profile

B2B SaaS companies with field sales, regional account executives, event teams, partner teams, local business development reps, or vertical-specific outside sales motions.

Best-fit teams have offline activity that creates meetings, demos, partner opportunities, or regional pipeline before standard CRM opportunity creation.

### Pain Points

- Field events and local account visits are hard to connect to pipeline quality.
- Reps attend events or visit accounts without consistent learning.
- Regional territory work is often tracked only after meetings are booked.
- Managers lack visibility into offline demand creation.
- Partner or channel field activity can be hard to compare.

### Sales Process

Position Prometheus as field-sourced pipeline intelligence. Discovery should map offline demand generation, field events, regional account visits, partner activity, meeting value, and CRM handoff.

### Decision Makers

- VP sales.
- VP revenue.
- Field marketing leader.
- Regional sales director.
- Sales operations leader.
- Partnerships leader.

### Buying Triggers

- Expansion into regional or vertical field motion.
- Need to measure event and field ROI.
- Low conversion from field activity to demos.
- Sales leadership wants better territory activity insight.
- Partner or channel teams need operating discipline.

### Prometheus IQ Value Proposition

Prometheus helps B2B SaaS teams connect offline field activity to qualified meetings, demos, partner opportunities, and territory learning before CRM opportunities appear.

### Pilot Strategy

Pilot around one region, event series, or vertical field team for 30 days. Track account visits, event interactions, partner touches, qualified meetings, demos scheduled, follow-ups, and source categories.

### Enterprise Rollout Strategy

Expand by region, vertical, event program, or partner channel. Standardize field-sourced meeting definitions, event categories, account tiers, and CRM handoff expectations.

### ROI Discussion

Discuss ROI through qualified meetings, demos, pipeline influence, event productivity, reduced unproductive field spend, and better regional account targeting.

### Objection Handling

- "We already have CRM and marketing attribution." Response: Prometheus captures field execution and learning before standard attribution sees an opportunity.
- "Our sales motion is mostly digital." Response: qualify carefully; Prometheus fits only where physical field activity matters.
- "Attribution is complex." Response: start with leading field indicators and avoid overclaiming revenue attribution.

### Success Metrics

- Field interactions logged.
- Qualified meetings.
- Demos scheduled.
- Partner opportunities.
- Follow-ups completed.
- Productive events or account tiers.
- Pipeline influenced.
- Manager review usefulness.

## Cross-Industry Qualification Rule

An industry is qualified for active Sales pursuit only when all five conditions are true:

1. The customer has recurring outside-sales activity.
2. The field outcome can be measured.
3. A manager or operator can enforce adoption.
4. A controlled pilot can be scoped without production-system changes.
5. A successful pilot has a credible paid rollout path.

If any condition is missing, keep the industry in research or nurture until the gap is resolved.
