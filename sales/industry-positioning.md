# Industry Positioning

Purpose: give Prometheus reusable positioning by industry while keeping the core category consistent.

## Universal Positioning

Prometheus Route IQ helps outside sales teams know where to go, verify what happened, learn which field activity produces, and improve the next route.

Universal pains:

- Reps guess where to spend time.
- Managers cannot see the quality of field activity.
- CRM data starts too late.
- Route planning ignores sales outcomes.
- Good rep knowledge stays in individual memory.
- Field activity is hard to coach and scale.

Universal promise:

- Sell more.
- Drive less.
- Waste less time.
- Learn what works.
- Make tomorrow's route smarter.

## Industry Discovery Add-Ons

Healthcare:

- Who are the referral sources?
- What compliance or patient privacy rules govern field notes?
- What counts as a qualified referral relationship?

Medical devices:

- Which stakeholders matter before an opportunity is created?
- How are territory visits planned today?
- What evidence matters to sales leadership?

Solar and roofing:

- How are neighborhoods selected?
- What weather, seasonality, or home-profile signals matter?
- How are appointments verified?

Pest control, telecom, security:

- What campaign areas are active?
- What door or visit outcomes are tracked?
- How is rep activity reviewed?

Payroll, uniforms, commercial cleaning:

- Which business types are best fit?
- How are local business visits sequenced?
- What counts as a qualified decision-maker interaction?

Industrial, manufacturing, logistics:

- Which account types or facilities are worth field visits?
- How are territories assigned?
- What pipeline stage begins after a field visit?

Insurance and B2B SaaS:

- What offline field activity creates qualified appointments?
- How does field work connect to downstream pipeline?
- What local events or account clusters matter?

## Messaging By Buyer

Owner or CEO:

- Prometheus turns field sales from guessing into a measurable operating system.

Sales leader:

- Prometheus shows which field activities create qualified outcomes and where reps should focus next.

Operations leader:

- Prometheus reduces field execution ambiguity and creates a repeatable review cadence.

Manager:

- Prometheus gives practical visibility into visits, outcomes, and coaching opportunities.

Rep:

- Prometheus helps you decide where to go and prove what you did without burying you in admin work.

IT/security/legal:

- Prometheus pilots should use clear access, data, privacy, and user-consent boundaries.

## Positioning Boundaries

- Do not claim industry-specific compliance readiness unless verified.
- Do not imply customer systems are integrated unless they are.
- Do not promise automated route optimization beyond current product behavior.
- Do not present gym terminology as universal.
- Do not position Prometheus as replacing sales management.
