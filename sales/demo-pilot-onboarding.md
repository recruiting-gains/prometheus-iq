# Demo, Pilot, And Onboarding

Purpose: define how Sales moves a qualified prospect from demo to controlled pilot to customer onboarding.

## Demo Methodology

The demo should show Prometheus as an operating system for field execution, not a screen tour.

Demo principle:

`Show the buyer how decisions improve from field data.`

## Standard Demo Flow

### 1. Confirm Context

Restate the discovery finding:

- Team:
- Field outcome:
- Current problem:
- Pilot success metric:

Ask for confirmation before showing the product.

### 2. Rep Day Planning

Show how a rep answers:

- Where should I go today?
- Which locations are worth visiting?
- Which stops are proven versus discovery?
- What does success look like for this field session?

### 3. Visit Execution

Show how a rep logs:

- Location.
- Time window.
- Conversations or attempts.
- Leads, appointments, inspections, units, or qualified outcomes.
- Follow-up.
- Notes.
- Verification where appropriate.

### 4. Manager Visibility

Show how a manager answers:

- Who was active?
- What happened in the field?
- Which areas or categories produced?
- What should change tomorrow?
- Which reps need coaching or support?

### 5. Learning Loop

Show how Prometheus converts field history into better routing, better category choices, and better management review.

### 6. Pilot Ask

Close the demo by proposing a specific controlled pilot:

- Users.
- Timeline.
- Scope.
- Success metrics.
- Review cadence.
- Required approvals.
- Next decision date.

## Demo Rules

- Do not show internal-only Founder Companion materials.
- Do not expose source code, secrets, scoring internals, or private customer data.
- Do not imply integrations are live unless verified.
- Do not demo unsupported industry-specific workflows as current product behavior.
- Do not present Fitness Connection-specific materials as generic customer assets without context.

## Pilot Structure

Default pilot duration:

- 14 days for small controlled validation.
- 30 days for regional or enterprise evaluation.
- 60 to 90 days only when procurement, training, or cross-market deployment requires it.

Pilot roles:

- Executive sponsor: approves evaluation and receives outcome report.
- Operating owner: manages daily adoption.
- Pilot users: perform field activity and log outcomes.
- Sales lead: manages communication and commercial path.
- Customer success lead: manages onboarding, support, and adoption.
- Product/engineering contact: receives validated product issues only.

Pilot scope must include:

- Approved users.
- Approved use case.
- Start date and end date.
- Data entry rules.
- Privacy and HR boundaries.
- Success metrics.
- Review cadence.
- Support channel.
- Stop conditions.
- Final decision criteria.

## Pilot Onboarding

### Day 0

- Confirm approval in writing.
- Confirm users and manager.
- Confirm allowed data.
- Confirm security and privacy expectations.
- Confirm access path.
- Confirm success metrics.
- Run one practice log.
- Schedule weekly review.

### User Training

Training should take 20 to 30 minutes.

Agenda:

- Why the pilot exists.
- What reps do each field day.
- How to review route guidance.
- How to log a visit.
- What not to enter.
- When to request support.
- One practice workflow.

### Manager Training

Manager training should take 30 minutes.

Agenda:

- What Prometheus will and will not prove.
- What metrics to review.
- How to interpret activity versus outcomes.
- How to coach without treating the tool as surveillance.
- Weekly review format.
- Pilot stop conditions.

## Customer Onboarding

Customer onboarding begins only after a paid deployment or approved expanded pilot.

Required onboarding outputs:

- Customer operating profile.
- Role list.
- User list.
- Industry terminology map.
- Success metrics.
- Review cadence.
- Support process.
- Adoption scorecard.
- Executive reporting format.
- Renewal checkpoint date.

## Pilot To Paid Conversion

The conversion conversation should be based on evidence:

- Adoption: did users use the workflow?
- Activity: did field visibility improve?
- Outcomes: did the customer create more qualified output or improve productivity?
- Learning: did the team learn where to focus?
- Management value: did managers make better decisions?
- Expansion readiness: is there a larger team, market, or region that can use it?

Conversion close:

`The pilot showed [evidence]. The recommended next step is [paid scope] for [users/market] over [term], measured by [renewal metrics].`

## Handoff Boundaries

Sales may request:

- Customer-facing demo copy.
- Approved pilot setup.
- Industry terminology mapping.
- Onboarding collateral.

Sales may not directly modify:

- Dashboard code.
- CRM implementation.
- Companion behavior.
- Infrastructure.
- Integrations.
- Intelligence Engine scoring.
