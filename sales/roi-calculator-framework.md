# ROI Calculator Framework

Purpose: define how Sales estimates Prometheus value across outside-sales industries without inventing results.

## ROI Principle

Prometheus ROI should be based on measured field-sales improvement, not generic software productivity claims.

Use conservative ranges until customer-specific data is verified.

## ROI Equation

Monthly estimated value:

`incremental qualified outcomes x value per qualified outcome`

minus:

`software cost + onboarding/support cost + operational cost`

Potential value sources:

- More qualified outcomes.
- Higher conversion from field activity.
- Less wasted travel time.
- Lower fuel or mileage cost.
- Faster rep ramp.
- Better manager coaching.
- Better territory coverage.
- Reduced manual reporting.

## Conservative Calculation

Use three cases:

Low case:

- 5% improvement in qualified outcomes or field efficiency.
- 25% adoption among assigned users.
- No expansion assumption.

Base case:

- 10% improvement in qualified outcomes or field efficiency.
- 50% adoption among assigned users.
- Stable manager review cadence.

High case:

- 20% improvement in qualified outcomes or field efficiency.
- 70% or higher adoption.
- Expansion-ready manager usage.

## Pilot ROI Scorecard

During pilot, collect:

- Assigned users.
- Active users.
- Field days logged.
- Visits logged.
- Qualified outcomes.
- Follow-ups created.
- Follow-ups completed.
- Best categories or account types.
- Worst categories or account types.
- Estimated time saved.
- Manager review usefulness.
- User friction.

Pilot ROI should separate:

- Verified results: measured in pilot.
- Estimated value: calculated from verified results and customer-provided values.
- Assumptions: not verified yet.
- Unknowns: missing data.

## ROI Conversation Structure

1. Establish current baseline.
2. Identify one or two costly inefficiencies.
3. Estimate the value of improving those inefficiencies.
4. Run pilot to verify adoption and leading indicators.
5. Convert verified evidence into a paid deployment business case.

## Red Lines

- Do not guarantee revenue lift.
- Do not use unapproved customer data in external claims.
- Do not hide assumptions.
- Do not count the same value twice.
- Do not claim ROI if the customer did not adopt the workflow.
- Do not use enterprise ROI language for a small pilot without sufficient data.
