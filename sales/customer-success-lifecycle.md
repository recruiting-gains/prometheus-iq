# Customer Success Lifecycle

Purpose: connect Sales, pilot operations, onboarding, expansion, and renewal without taking ownership of product implementation.

## Lifecycle Stages

### 1. Qualified Opportunity

Exit criteria:

- Field-sales problem is verified.
- Buyer and operating owner are identified.
- Pilot success can be measured.
- Risk concerns are known.

Primary owner: Sales.

### 2. Demo Accepted

Exit criteria:

- Buyer understands Prometheus as field-sales operating system.
- Pilot ask is clear.
- Required reviewers are known.
- No unsupported feature promises were made.

Primary owner: Sales.

### 3. Pilot Approved

Exit criteria:

- Written approval.
- Users, timeline, data rules, and success metrics confirmed.
- Review cadence scheduled.
- Support channel defined.

Primary owner: Sales and customer success.

### 4. Pilot Active

Exit criteria:

- Users onboarded.
- Manager onboarded.
- Field activity is being logged.
- Weekly review completed.
- Issues tracked without uncontrolled scope expansion.

Primary owner: customer success.

### 5. Pilot Reviewed

Exit criteria:

- Final pilot summary completed.
- Adoption and outcome evidence reviewed.
- Customer decision documented.
- Paid scope or no-go reason captured.

Primary owner: Sales.

### 6. Customer Onboarded

Exit criteria:

- Paid or approved deployment scope is active.
- Users and managers trained.
- Reporting cadence is running.
- Success metrics are baselined.
- Renewal checkpoint is scheduled.

Primary owner: customer success.

### 7. Expansion Qualified

Exit criteria:

- Current deployment is stable.
- Measurable value exists.
- New team, territory, region, or business unit has a similar field-sales problem.
- Sponsor supports expansion.

Primary owner: Sales and customer success.

### 8. Renewal Prepared

Exit criteria:

- Usage data reviewed.
- Outcomes summarized.
- Support issues resolved or explained.
- Expansion opportunities identified.
- Renewal recommendation drafted.

Primary owner: customer success and Sales.

## Success Metrics

Prometheus should measure value across four levels.

### Rep Metrics

- Active field days.
- Visits logged.
- Verified visits where applicable.
- Conversations or attempts.
- Leads, appointments, inspections, units, or qualified outcomes.
- Follow-ups created and completed.
- Field time or session productivity.
- Outcomes per visit.

### Manager Metrics

- Rep adoption rate.
- Visit completeness.
- Route compliance or route usefulness.
- High-performing categories or account types.
- Low-performing categories or account types.
- Time-window performance.
- Coaching opportunities identified.

### Business Metrics

- Qualified output per rep.
- Output per field day.
- Cost per qualified outcome.
- Revenue or pipeline influence.
- Wasted route time reduced.
- Field coverage improved.
- Conversion rate from field activity to downstream outcome.

### Strategic Metrics

- Confidence in where to deploy reps.
- Repeatability across teams or markets.
- Manager dependency on Prometheus reporting.
- Executive willingness to expand or renew.

## Expansion Strategy

Expansion should follow operating proof, not enthusiasm.

Expansion sequence:

1. Same team, more users.
2. Same market, adjacent territories.
3. Same region, additional managers.
4. New region with similar field motion.
5. Enterprise rollout with standardized onboarding and governance.

Expansion triggers:

- 70% or higher weekly active usage among assigned reps.
- Manager reviews Prometheus data weekly.
- At least one measurable field-sales improvement.
- Sponsor can name the next team or market.
- Support load is manageable.

Expansion blockers:

- Low usage.
- Unclear success metrics.
- Product issues blocking basic workflow.
- No manager adoption.
- No executive sponsor.
- Privacy, HR, legal, or security risk unresolved.

## Renewal Strategy

Renewal should begin early.

Renewal timeline:

- 90 days before renewal: review usage, outcomes, risks, and expansion path.
- 60 days before renewal: executive business review.
- 45 days before renewal: commercial proposal.
- 30 days before renewal: procurement/legal close.

Renewal narrative:

- What problem Prometheus was hired to solve.
- What changed operationally.
- What measurable value exists.
- What adoption proves durability.
- What should improve next term.
- Whether expansion is justified.

Renewal risks to monitor:

- Usage decline.
- Manager turnover.
- No executive review.
- Product issue backlog.
- Metrics not tied to buyer priorities.
- Customer sees Prometheus as optional reporting instead of operating rhythm.

## Customer Success Workflow

Weekly customer success review:

- Adoption.
- Activity.
- Outcomes.
- User friction.
- Manager feedback.
- Product issues.
- Commercial risks.
- Next action.

Monthly business review:

- Field production trends.
- Best performing territories, locations, or categories.
- Rep and manager adoption.
- Operational lessons.
- ROI estimate.
- Expansion readiness.
- Renewal confidence.

## Handoff To Other Subsystems

Create a future subsystem handoff only when customer evidence requires a product, CRM, integration, infrastructure, or intelligence change.

Handoff must include:

- Customer name or anonymized label.
- Evidence source.
- Business impact.
- Requested behavior.
- Current workaround.
- Urgency.
- Owning subsystem.

Do not convert one customer's preference into product roadmap without repeated evidence or explicit founder decision.
