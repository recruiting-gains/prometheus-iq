# Prometheus Company Identity v1

## Company Mission

Prometheus exists to make outside sales intelligent, directed, accountable, and compounding. Its mission is to help organizations that sell in the physical world know where to go, why to go there, what happened, and what should happen next.

## Company Vision

Prometheus is building the AI operating system for outside sales. Over time, Prometheus should become the intelligence layer behind every field sales motion: routing, territory learning, visit verification, opportunity discovery, follow-up, coaching, performance insight, and executive visibility.

Prometheus IQ is the flagship product. It is the first expression of the company's larger operating system vision.

## Core Values

1. Field reality over boardroom theory. Prometheus is built from real selling conditions, not abstract CRM assumptions.
2. Intelligence before activity. More motion is not the goal. Better-directed motion is.
3. Proof without surveillance. Prometheus verifies intentional work without becoming a background tracking system.
4. Compounding learning. Every visit, result, and missed opportunity should improve the next decision.
5. Enterprise trust. Security, clarity, governance, and operational discipline matter from the beginning.
6. Founder-led courage. Prometheus favors direct observation, fast learning, and principled execution over trend-chasing.

## Company Principles

Prometheus is not a single-feature app. It is not a route planner, CRM plugin, gym sales tool, or GPS tracker. It is an operating system for outside sales.

Prometheus should simplify the field rep's day while increasing leadership visibility. It should help managers understand reality without forcing reps into excessive admin work. It should make field sales more effective without stripping away human judgment.

Prometheus should protect its intelligence layer. Customers deserve clear outcomes and trustworthy controls, but the company should not expose its full recommendation logic casually.

## Founder Story

Prometheus was born from lived field sales experience: wasted miles, dead zones, unclear priorities, inconsistent follow-up, and the frustration of being told to "go generate business" without intelligent direction.

The founder saw that outside sales professionals were expected to create demand in the real world while most systems only recorded activity after the fact. Prometheus was created to solve that gap.

Gym sales is the first production validation environment because it provides real-world field motion, local prospecting, route pressure, and measurable outcomes. It is not the company's category.

## Why Prometheus Exists

Prometheus exists because outside sales remains one of the most economically important and under-intelligenced parts of business. Companies spend heavily on CRMs, dashboards, and pipeline tools, yet the rep in the field often still lacks a clear answer to the most important question: where should I go next?

Prometheus exists to turn field activity into an intelligent operating loop.

## Problems Prometheus Solves

Prometheus solves poor territory direction, wasted travel time, low visibility into field effort, weak follow-up discipline, inconsistent prospect discovery, unreliable activity proof, and lost institutional knowledge.

It helps organizations connect field behavior to business outcomes without relying only on memory, screenshots, manual reporting, or end-of-day CRM cleanup.

## Who Prometheus Serves

Prometheus serves any organization that relies on outside sales professionals: healthcare, medical devices, solar, roofing, HVAC, security, commercial cleaning, telecommunications, payroll, insurance, industrial sales, manufacturing, logistics, financial services, construction, and any business where sales happen outside the office.

The customer is not "gyms." The customer is the outside sales organization.

## What Prometheus Believes

Prometheus believes the physical world still matters. Relationships, visits, neighborhoods, timing, and human presence still drive revenue. AI should not replace outside salespeople; it should make them sharper, better directed, and more effective.

## What Prometheus Refuses To Become

Prometheus refuses to become a shallow trend product, a surveillance company, a generic CRM clone, a single-industry niche tool, or a feature set that can be copied without understanding the field.

Prometheus will not optimize its identity around short-term hype, investor fashion, or the first validation market.

## What Customers Should Feel

Customers should feel directed, confident, and in control. Reps should feel supported rather than watched. Managers should feel they can finally see field execution clearly. Executives should feel that outside sales is becoming measurable without becoming mechanical.

## What Investors Should Understand

Investors should understand that Prometheus is not selling routing software. It is building infrastructure for outside sales intelligence. The market is broad, the first validation environment is narrow by design, and the defensibility comes from accumulated field data, workflow adoption, operating discipline, and decision intelligence.

## What Employees Should Believe

Employees should believe they are building a durable company, not chasing a category gimmick. Their job is to protect clarity, serve field reality, build enterprise-grade systems, and preserve the long-term identity of Prometheus.

## What Competitors Should Fear

Competitors should fear that Prometheus understands the field from the inside. They should fear the company's ability to learn from real sales motion, compound intelligence over time, and expand across industries without losing its core identity.
