# Smoothie Depot Daily Operating Checklists v1

Workspace: Smoothie Depot  
Operating Profile: Franchise Development  
Audience: Cruz only

## Morning Checklist

- Open Smoothie Depot Founder Dashboard.
- Confirm workspace is `smoothie_depot`.
- Confirm operating profile is `franchise_development`.
- Confirm no cross-workspace data appears.
- Review Today's Mission.
- Review new business prospects.
- Review follow-ups due today.
- Review JR handoffs.
- Review commission forecast.
- Set today's Top 5 priorities.

## Research Checklist

- Select business from the current Smoothie Depot prospect queue.
- Generate or open Executive Intelligence Dossier.
- Confirm lawful public sources only.
- Review executive summary.
- Review confidence level.
- Review top unknowns.
- Review Smoothie Depot strategic fit.
- Review suggested outreach angle.
- Decide: Call / Research further / Archive.

## Call Checklist

- Review Executive Summary.
- Review opening angle.
- Review top discovery questions.
- Review verification checklist.
- Review conversation strategy.
- Confirm public contact method.
- Make call.
- Record outcome immediately.
- Update stage language.
- Create next action.

## Discovery Checklist

- Confirm decision-maker authority.
- Confirm interest type.
- Confirm current business context.
- Confirm expansion interest.
- Confirm Smoothie Depot fit.
- Confirm timeline.
- Confirm number of locations desired.
- Confirm territory interest.
- Confirm capital discussion willingness.
- Schedule next step or classify out.

## Qualification Checklist

- Run Franchise Readiness Assessment.
- Capture liquidity.
- Capture net worth.
- Capture timeline.
- Capture number of locations.
- Capture operating experience.
- Capture territory interest.
- Capture risk concerns.
- Decide: Qualified for JR / Continue discovery / Nurture / Not qualified.

## JR Handoff Checklist

- Prepare Executive Brief.
- Prepare Evidence Summary.
- List Unknowns.
- List Verification items.
- Add Conversation notes.
- Add Next actions.
- Confirm readiness assessment status.
- Confirm commission forecast status.
- Confirm protected candidate status if applicable.
- Send or prepare for JR only after qualification is defensible.

## Follow-Up Checklist

- Review outstanding follow-ups.
- Review pending discovery.
- Review pending JR meetings.
- Review pending documents.
- Review no-response prospects.
- Review stalled qualified candidates.
- Review protection or commission windows.
- Set next action for every active prospect.

## End-Of-Day Checklist

- Count businesses researched.
- Count calls made.
- Count discovery meetings.
- Count qualified candidates.
- Count JR handoffs.
- Review projected commissions.
- Record major wins.
- Record blockers.
- Record new unknowns.
- Record tomorrow's Top 5 priorities.
- Decide tomorrow's starting mode: Research / Calls / Follow-up / Qualification / JR handoff.
