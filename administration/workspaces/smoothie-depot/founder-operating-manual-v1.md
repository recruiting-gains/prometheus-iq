# Smoothie Depot Founder Operating Manual v1

Workspace: Smoothie Depot  
Workspace ID: `smoothie_depot`  
Operating Profile: Franchise Development  
Owner: Prometheus Administration  
Audience: Cruz only  
Purpose: daily operating procedure so Cruz never has to wonder what to do next.

## Operating Rule

Prometheus is Cruz's operating system for this workspace. The day starts in the Smoothie Depot Founder Dashboard, moves through research, outreach, qualification, JR handoff preparation, follow-up, and ends with a written operating summary.

This manual uses the existing platform only:

- Dashboard for daily status and workspace visibility.
- Research for Executive Intelligence Dossiers and prospect discovery.
- CRM planning contracts for stage language, follow-ups, JR handoffs, and commission tracking.
- Intelligence planning contracts for Franchise Readiness Assessment and Business Acquisition Intelligence.
- Shared lead intake for approved workspace lead submissions.

No new subsystem, engine, CRM runtime, Dashboard runtime, Intelligence runtime, or production workflow is created by this manual.

This manual applies only to Smoothie Depot Workspace.

- Fitness Connection workflows.

## Daily Operating Loop

1. Start the day in the Smoothie Depot Founder Dashboard.
2. Decide Today's Mission.
3. Pull the current prospect queue.
4. Research only enough to make the next decision.
5. Call the highest-value prospects first.
6. Update the candidate stage after every call.
7. Run Franchise Readiness Assessment when interest exists.
8. Prepare JR handoff only when qualification evidence supports it.
9. Review follow-ups before the day ends.
10. Write tomorrow's priorities before stopping.

## Morning Startup

### 1. Open Dashboard

Open the Smoothie Depot Founder Dashboard.

Confirm:

- Workspace is Smoothie Depot.
- Operating profile is Franchise Development.
- Data mode remains workspace fixture or approved workspace data.
- No cross-workspace data appears.

### 2. Review Today's Mission

Write one sentence:

Today I am trying to move Smoothie Depot franchise development forward by:

- finding better prospects;
- contacting qualified operators;
- qualifying interested candidates;
- preparing JR handoffs;
- protecting commission opportunities.

Pick one primary mission for the day.

### 3. Review New Business Prospects

Open the current Research prospect list.

For each new prospect, check:

- business name;
- category;
- public contact method;
- confidence;
- reason it may fit Smoothie Depot;
- suggested first outreach angle;
- unknowns that would change the decision.

Do not call from a weak profile unless the opportunity cost is acceptable.

### 4. Review Follow-Ups

Review:

- follow-ups due today;
- no-response prospects;
- pending discovery answers;
- candidates waiting on documents;
- candidates waiting for JR action.

Any follow-up with an active candidate outranks new research unless today's mission explicitly says otherwise.

### 5. Review JR Handoffs

Review every candidate near JR handoff.

Confirm:

- readiness assessment exists;
- liquidity and net worth are captured or marked unknown;
- territory interest is clear;
- number of locations desired is captured;
- conversation notes are current;
- open verification items are listed;
- next action is explicit.

### 6. Review Commission Forecast

Review:

- protected candidate list;
- active JR handoffs;
- forecast amount;
- forecast basis;
- forecast confidence;
- trigger stage;
- expiration or review date.

Commission forecast is a planning signal, not a guarantee.

### 7. Establish Today's Top 5 Priorities

Write five priorities in order:

1. highest-value revenue or candidate movement;
2. required follow-up;
3. best new outreach;
4. highest-value research gap;
5. administrative closeout item.

If more than five items exist, defer the rest.

## Research Workflow

For each business:

### 1. Generate Executive Intelligence Dossier

Use the Smoothie Depot Executive Due Diligence Intelligence Standard.

Required sections before serious outreach:

- executive summary;
- business identity;
- public owner/operator footprint;
- reputation intelligence;
- market position;
- franchise compatibility;
- Smoothie Depot strategic fit;
- conversation preparation;
- unknowns register;
- verification checklist;
- recommended next actions.

### 2. Review Confidence

Classify confidence:

- High: multiple reliable public sources and no material contradiction.
- Medium-high: credible evidence with key operating or intent unknowns.
- Medium: plausible but limited sources or one major assumption.
- Low: speculative; do not prioritize unless strategically necessary.

### 3. Review Unknowns

Rank unknowns by business value:

- decision-maker identity;
- second-concept interest;
- available liquidity;
- net worth;
- timeline;
- number of locations desired;
- territory interest;
- whether Smoothie Depot is complementary or competitive;
- whether JR should see the candidate.

### 4. Decide

Choose one:

- Call: evidence is strong enough and the outreach angle is clear.
- Research further: one missing fact materially changes the call quality.
- Archive: fit is weak, evidence is thin, or timing is not worth the call.

## Outreach Workflow

For every call, review before dialing:

- Executive Summary.
- Opening angle.
- Top discovery questions.
- Verification checklist.
- Conversation strategy.

### Call Preparation

Prepare the call in this order:

1. Why this business.
2. Why Smoothie Depot may fit.
3. What Cruz needs to verify.
4. What next step is appropriate if interest exists.
5. What disqualifies the prospect.

### Opening Angle

Use public business context. Do not overstate evidence.

Example structure:

I saw that your business already serves health, food, wellness, hospitality, or local customer traffic. I am working on Smoothie Depot franchise development in Houston and wanted to see whether a franchise or second-concept conversation is worth exploring.

### Top Discovery Questions

Ask:

- Are you open to franchise or second-concept opportunities?
- Are you thinking about expansion in Houston or nearby markets?
- Would a beverage-forward concept fit your current customer base?
- Are you exploring one location or multiple locations?
- What timeline would make sense if the fit was real?
- What would you need to know before taking a Smoothie Depot conversation seriously?

### Verification Checklist

Verify:

- decision-maker authority;
- current business role;
- expansion interest;
- liquidity willingness to discuss;
- timeline;
- territory interest;
- operating experience;
- number of locations desired;
- willingness to speak with JR.

### Conversation Strategy

The goal is not to sell everything on the first call.

The goal is to classify:

- no interest;
- research more;
- follow up later;
- interested but not qualified;
- discovery needed;
- qualified for JR handoff.

### After Call

Immediately update CRM stage language:

- Business Identified.
- Owner Research Needed.
- Contact Found.
- Outreach Sent.
- Interested.
- Discovery Scheduled.
- Discovery Complete.
- Qualified Candidate.
- JR Handoff.
- FDD / Review.
- Closed Won.
- Not Qualified.
- No Response.

If CRM runtime is not approved, record the update in the workspace operating notes or current manual tracking artifact using the same stage language.

## Qualification Workflow

If interested, run Franchise Readiness Assessment.

Collect:

- liquidity;
- net worth;
- timeline;
- number of locations;
- operating experience;
- territory interest;
- decision-maker authority;
- current business obligations;
- risk concerns;
- best next step.

### Qualification Decision

Classify:

- Qualified for JR: interest, capital signal, timeline, territory interest, and operator credibility are strong enough.
- Continue discovery: interest exists but one or more material unknowns remain.
- Nurture: fit may exist later but timing or readiness is weak.
- Not qualified: capital, interest, authority, or fit does not support continuation.

## JR Handoff Workflow

When qualified, prepare:

- Executive Brief.
- Evidence Summary.
- Unknowns.
- Verification items.
- Conversation notes.
- Next actions.

### Executive Brief

Include:

- candidate name and business;
- why the candidate may fit Smoothie Depot;
- current stage;
- readiness summary;
- capital self-report summary;
- desired number of locations;
- territory interest;
- timeline;
- risk flags;
- recommended JR ask.

### Evidence Summary

Include only verified or properly labeled evidence:

- public evidence;
- owner-provided facts;
- self-reported financial readiness;
- unknowns;
- assumptions.

### Verification Items

List exactly what JR or Cruz must still verify before serious commitment:

- liquidity proof path;
- net worth proof path;
- territory rights;
- franchise economics;
- legal/FDD readiness;
- operator involvement;
- protection/commission expectations.

### Conversation Notes

Capture:

- why the candidate advanced;
- what they said;
- what they resisted;
- what they asked for;
- what Cruz promised;
- what happens next.

### Next Actions

Every JR handoff must end with one clear next action:

- JR call;
- Cruz follow-up;
- send information;
- request documents;
- schedule discovery;
- pause;
- archive.

## Follow-Up Workflow

Every day, review:

- outstanding follow-ups;
- pending discovery;
- pending JR meetings;
- pending documents;
- no-response prospects;
- stalled qualified candidates;
- protection or commission windows.

Follow-up priority:

1. JR-related commitments.
2. Qualified or near-qualified candidates.
3. Discovery scheduled or waiting.
4. Interested but incomplete.
5. No-response prospects.
6. Research-only prospects.

Never end the day with an interested prospect lacking a next action.

## End Of Day

Dashboard or operating notes should summarize:

- businesses researched;
- calls made;
- discovery meetings;
- qualified candidates;
- JR handoffs;
- projected commissions;
- tomorrow's priorities.

### End-Of-Day Closeout

Before stopping:

1. Count work completed.
2. Update every touched prospect stage.
3. Record follow-ups.
4. Record new unknowns.
5. Record commission forecast changes.
6. Record tomorrow's Top 5.
7. Decide whether tomorrow starts with research, calls, follow-up, qualification, or JR handoff.

## Daily Decision Rule

If Cruz is unsure what to do next, use this order:

1. Complete overdue follow-ups.
2. Move interested prospects toward qualification.
3. Prepare qualified candidates for JR.
4. Call highest-ranked prospects.
5. Research the highest-value unknown blocking a call.
6. Update Dashboard/CRM planning notes.
7. Write tomorrow's Top 5.
