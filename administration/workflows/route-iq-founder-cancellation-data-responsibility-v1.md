# Route IQ Cancellation And Data Responsibility v1

1. Verify request authority and approved cancellation terms.
2. Record the requested and authorized effective dates separately.
3. Suspend access without deleting evidence.
4. Reconcile billing through an approved process; do not promise refunds or credits.
5. Identify export, retention, legal hold, deletion, and return obligations.
6. Keep company/workspace isolation throughout disposition.
7. Require Data-owner execution and evidence for any mutation.
8. Confirm access closure, financial disposition, customer communication, and retained obligations.

Unknown legal, payment, or data responsibility is `FOUNDER DECISION REQUIRED`; it is not resolved
by cancelling access.
