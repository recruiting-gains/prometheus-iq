# Route IQ Founder Revenue Operations v1

1. Verify the commercial-model and decision-register versions.
2. Keep new inquiries in Draft until required intake evidence validates.
3. Confirm customer authority, legal review, order form, data terms, support ownership, and
   workspace validation before readiness for activation.
4. Confirm entity, tax, bank, pricing, billing-provider, payment, agreement, and cancellation
   evidence before live revenue.
5. Record safe evidence references and exact lifecycle transition; never record credentials.
6. Reconcile customer status, invoice status, access status, and support ownership separately.
7. Stop on mismatch. Never infer approval from payment intent, conversation, or CRM stage.
