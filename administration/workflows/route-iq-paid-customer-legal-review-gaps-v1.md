- term, renewal, suspension, termination, cancellation, and post-termination access;
- warranties, disclaimers, indemnities, liability limits, governing law, and dispute process;

- privacy roles, processing instructions, security commitments, subprocessors, incidents,

- service commitments, support hours, maintenance, business continuity, and force majeure;
- order-form precedence, amendment, assignment, notices, signatures, and entire agreement;
- pilot conversion, non-production limitations, and data disposition if a pilot form is used.

No template in this repository is legal advice, approved customer paper, or permission to sign,
invoice, charge, activate, or process customer data.
