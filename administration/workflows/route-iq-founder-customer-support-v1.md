# Route IQ Founder Customer Support v1

1. Receive the issue through the approved support channel and assign a safe issue ID.
2. Verify company/workspace, role class, release identity, observed behavior, and impact without
   collecting credentials or unnecessary customer data.
3. Separate verified fact, customer statement, inference, and unknown.
4. Treat unknown isolation, authorization, security, billing, or data-integrity impact as critical.
5. Contain safely: stop new activation or the affected workflow without deleting customer data.
6. Route to the owning subsystem and set the next customer update time.
7. Do not promise features, refunds, credits, service levels, data changes, or legal outcomes.
8. Close only after owner verification, regression evidence, customer-safe communication, and
   reconciliation of access, billing, and data responsibility.

Support must not reveal another company, Founder-only intelligence, security internals, or
credential material. A customer report never authorizes production mutation.
