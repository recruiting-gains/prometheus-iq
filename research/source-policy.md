# Research Source Policy

## Core Standard

Research outputs must be attributable, reviewable, and honest about uncertainty.

Every meaningful claim should be traceable to either:

- A cited external source.
- A repository file path.
- A clearly marked hypothesis.
- A clearly marked founder assumption.

## Source Trust Tiers

### Tier 1: Primary

Preferred for facts that drive strategy or handoffs.

Examples:

- Official company pages and blogs.
- Product documentation.
- SEC filings and investor relations pages.
- Government agency publications.
- Patent databases.
- Academic papers.
- Official pricing pages.
- Official API documentation.

### Tier 2: Reputable Secondary

Useful for context, market framing, and corroboration.

Examples:

- Established business publications.
- Reputable technology publications.
- Industry analyst reports.
- Recognized trade publications.
- Conference recordings from identifiable organizations.

### Tier 3: Directional

Useful as weak signals only.

Examples:

- Startup databases.
- Lead/contact/company enrichment databases when used only for discovery or enrichment.
- Podcast interviews.
- Social posts from identifiable founders, executives, investors, or operators.
- Community discussions with corroboration.

## Permitted Enrichment Sources

Apollo is permitted as a Research enrichment source for franchise target research when available through approved ChatGPT plugins/connectors.

Apollo may be used for:

- Founder/operator discovery.
- Executive identification.
- Company enrichment.
- Industry filtering.
- Location filtering.
- Company size signals.
- Job title signals.
- Contact/persona discovery.
- Multi-location operator discovery.
- Restaurant, wellness, fitness, supplement, retail, food and beverage, consumer health, investor/operator, and franchise-related target discovery.

Apollo is not a source of truth. Treat Apollo-derived findings as enrichment signals unless corroborated by lawful public sources or owner-provided evidence.

Apollo-derived findings must be labeled as enrichment signals unless corroborated.

Apollo must not be used alone to make final ownership, capital readiness, franchise-fit, or executive decision conclusions.

Required operating rule:

- Apollo = lead/contact/company enrichment source.
- Research = verification, scoring, qualification probability, and Executive Intelligence Package creation.

Future franchise target lists must document whether Apollo was used. If Apollo was not used, Research must explain why.

Required Apollo target profiles for franchise-development research:

- Houston-area franchise operators.
- Restaurant group owners.
- Multi-location business owners.
- Fitness company founders.
- Wellness company founders.
- Supplement/nutrition retail owners.
- Food and beverage operators.
- Consumer health executives.
- Private investors with operating backgrounds.
- Business owners with expansion signals.

### Excluded

Do not use as evidence:

- Unsupported scraped sources.
- Anonymous claims without corroboration.
- Scraped private groups.
- Leaked documents.
- Credentialed or paywalled content accessed without permission.
- Customer private data.
- Production exports.

## Citation Requirements

Each external source entry should include:

- Title.
- Publisher.
- URL.
- Date published when available.
- Date retrieved when publication date is unavailable.
- Source tier.
- Relevant category.
- Notes on limitations.

## Fact And Hypothesis Labels

Use these labels consistently:

- `Verified fact`: directly supported by source evidence.
- `Hypothesis`: a reasoned interpretation that needs validation.
- `Unknown`: not enough evidence yet.
- `Founder assumption`: a working assumption from company strategy or founder context.

## Executive Intelligence Package Evidence Labels

Executive Intelligence Packages must use exactly one of these labels for every material conclusion:

- `Observed`: directly visible in a lawful public source.
- `Corroborated`: confirmed by more than one lawful public source.
- `Owner-provided`: provided directly by an authorized business representative.
- `Unknown`: not established by available lawful or owner-provided evidence.
- `Inference`: reasoned conclusion derived from labeled evidence.

Every Executive Intelligence Package recommendation and major conclusion must also include:

- Evidence basis.
- Source reliability.
- Confidence level.
- Verification needed.
- Business decision impact.

## Production Boundary

Research source collection must not:

- Read private production data unless explicitly provided for research review.
- Modify production data.
- Modify Dashboard, Companion, CRM, Infrastructure, Website, or Intelligence Engine.
- Publish public content without approval.
- Trigger external communication workflows.
- Use psychological profiling, hidden records, surveillance, restricted-platform scraping, or private business records for Executive Intelligence Packages.
