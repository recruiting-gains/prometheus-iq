## Mission

Produce the highest-quality lawful executive due diligence package possible before Cruz speaks to a Smoothie Depot franchise prospect.

Primary question:

If Prometheus had to brief the CEO of a Fortune 500 company before they met this business owner, what would that briefing contain?

## Evidence Classification

Every conclusion must be labeled as one of:

- Observed: directly visible in a lawful public source.
- Inferred from multiple evidence sources: supported by at least two lawful public sources.
- Self-reported: stated by the business or its official channels.
- Owner-provided: provided directly by the prospect during a conversation.
- Unknown: not established by available lawful sources.

Unknowns are not failures. Unknowns are decision intelligence and must be ranked by business value.

## Executive Challenge Questions

For every conclusion, Research must ask:

- Why do we believe this?
- What evidence supports it?
- What evidence contradicts it?
- How confident are we?
- What would change our conclusion?
- What are we assuming?
- What do we still not know?

Never stop at one explanation when the evidence can support multiple hypotheses.

## Second-Order Analysis Standard

Every major observation must produce:

- Observation.
- Business implication.
- Strategic implication.
- Conversation implication.
- Verification needed.
- Recommended action.

## Third-Order Analysis Standard

Every recommendation must explain how the observation changes:

- Outreach priority.
- Smoothie Depot fit.
- JR preparation.
- Discovery questions.
- Timeline.
- Follow-up.
- Expansion potential.

## Opportunity Cost Standard

Every ranked report must answer:

If Cruz only has time to call one business today, why should it be this one?

The answer must compare the recommended business against every currently known prospect and explain why each alternative is lower priority, higher uncertainty, less strategically useful, or better suited for later follow-up.

## Alternative Hypothesis Standard

Research must deliberately build alternative explanations.

Example:

- Primary hypothesis: Public evidence may indicate expansion.
- Alternative hypothesis: The business may be stabilizing rather than expanding.
- Required conclusion: State which hypothesis has stronger evidence and what would change the conclusion.

## Red Team Review Standard

Every report must attack itself:

- What if this recommendation is wrong?
- What evidence is weakest?
- What assumptions are unsupported?
- What would prove the conclusion false?
- Which prospect should move up if the top recommendation fails?

## Unknown Value Analysis Standard

Unknowns must be ranked by business value.

Required question:

If Cruz could only learn one additional fact before calling, which fact would improve the decision the most?

Repeat until all major unknowns are ranked.

## Decision Confidence Model

Every recommendation must include:

- Evidence Quality.
- Source Reliability.
- Confidence.
- Business Impact.
- Verification Priority.

Confidence levels:

- High: multiple reliable public sources support the conclusion and no material contradiction is known.
- Medium-high: credible public evidence supports the conclusion, but at least one key operating or intent unknown remains.
- Medium: the conclusion is plausible but relies on limited public sources or one major assumption.
- Low: the conclusion is speculative and should not guide outreach without more evidence.

## Scenario Decision Tree

Every executive dossier must include branching actions:

- If candidate confirms available liquidity: prepare franchise economics discussion and JR handoff.
- If candidate does not confirm liquidity: keep relationship warm and test partnership, referral, or future timing.
- If candidate wants multiple units: escalate to territory strategy and multi-unit readiness review.
- If candidate only wants one unit: test founder involvement, location thesis, and first-unit operating readiness.
- If timeline exceeds 12 months: downgrade outreach urgency and schedule future refresh.
- If candidate is competitor-protective: switch to operator intelligence and partnership learning, not conversion pressure.

## Decision Journal Framework

After every real conversation, record:

- Why outreach occurred.
- Predicted outcome.
- Actual outcome.
- Why prospect advanced.
- Why prospect paused.
- Why prospect closed.
- Why prospect declined.
- Which assumptions were correct.
- Which assumptions failed.
- What Prometheus should learn.
- How future recommendations should change.

Decision journals are future Research intelligence. CRM can track them only after an explicit approved CRM handoff.

## Quality Control Gate

Before finalizing a report, Research must ask:

- Is this the strongest evidence available?
- Have lawful public sources been exhausted for this decision stage?
- Have contradictions been identified?
- Have unknowns been prioritized?
- Would a private equity investment committee rely on this report?

If the answer is no, Research must either improve the report or explicitly label the gap and lower confidence.
