## Daily Founder Question

Who should I call today?

This workflow turns lawful public business information into a ranked Smoothie Depot franchise candidate call list. It is a research capability only. It does not write to CRM, Dashboard, Website, Infrastructure, Companion, Mobile, Route Intelligence, Active Pool, Fitness Connection, or the Intelligence Engine runtime.

## Allowed Public Sources

Use sources that are public, attributable, and lawful to review manually:

- Official business websites.
- Official location pages.
- Public chamber of commerce directories.
- Public tourism or district directories.
- Public restaurant, catering, and food-truck directories.
- Public news, trade, and local business reporting.
- Public franchise pages.
- Public review summaries when surfaced through unrestricted public pages.

Do not scrape unsupported or restricted sources. Do not bypass access controls. Do not collect private financials, credit reports, bank information, tax records, personal identity dossiers, private social data, or restricted-platform data.

## Data Capture Contract

Capture only public business information:

- Business name.
- Category.
- Public location.
- Public business phone.
- Public website or contact method.
- Google Business Profile, rating, and review count where surfaced by lawful public sources.
- Public review themes where available.
- Number of locations if publicly evident.
- Years operating if publicly evident.
- Public growth indicators.
- Reason it may be a good Smoothie Depot franchise prospect.
- Suggested first outreach angle.
- Confidence level.
- Evidence sources.

If a field is not available from a trustworthy unrestricted source, record it as not captured. Do not infer private facts.

## Executive Due Diligence Standard

Every report must meet the Smoothie Depot Executive Due Diligence Intelligence Standard v4 before it is treated as founder-ready.

Required executive review:

- Label every conclusion as observed, inferred from multiple evidence sources, self-reported, owner-provided, or unknown.
- Ask why each conclusion is believed, what supports it, what contradicts it, how confident Research is, what would change the conclusion, what is assumed, and what is still unknown.
- Produce competing hypotheses when evidence can support more than one explanation.
- Identify contradictions instead of smoothing them away.
- Rank unknowns by business value.
- Include a red-team review before finalizing.
- Include opportunity cost analysis answering why Cruz should call the top-ranked business if he only has time for one call.
- Include a scenario decision tree for liquidity, number of desired units, timeline, and competitive sensitivity.
- Prepare a post-meeting learning journal template before outreach.

Major observations must follow this chain:

Observation -> Business implication -> Strategic implication -> Conversation implication -> Verification needed -> Recommended action.

Every recommendation must explain how the observation changes outreach priority, Smoothie Depot fit, JR preparation, discovery questions, timeline, follow-up, and expansion potential.

## Scoring Model

Each prospect receives a 100-point opportunity score:

- Category fit: 20 points.
- Expansion or operator signal: 20 points.
- Contactability: 15 points.
- Public reputation and demand signal: 15 points.
- Franchise readiness hypothesis: 15 points.
- Evidence confidence and workspace safety: 15 points.

Priority bands:

- 85-100: Call today.
- 75-84: Call after top-priority prospects.
- 65-74: Research more or watchlist.
- Below 65: Do not prioritize yet.

## Daily Workflow

1. Select the geography. Current default: Houston and nearby Houston-area clusters, based on Smoothie Depot expansion research.
2. Build a target list from allowed public source categories.
3. Capture only business-level public information.
4. Separate verified public facts from hypotheses.
5. Score each prospect using the scoring model.
6. Assign a priority band and first outreach angle.
7. Save the founder-ready ranked list in the Smoothie Depot Research workspace.
8. Do not persist any record to CRM unless a future CRM handoff explicitly authorizes it.
9. Do not use Fitness Connection, Route Intelligence, Active Pool, unit, membership, or field-sales data.
10. Re-run validation before handoff.

## Outreach Guardrails

Outreach should use public business-level contact methods only. Do not use private personal contact information. Do not imply an existing relationship or affiliation. Do not present hypotheses as facts. Do not contact through restricted-platform scraping or automated direct messages.

Suggested call framing:

Prometheus Research identified the business as a public Houston-area operator with possible fit for a Smoothie Depot franchise development conversation. Cruz should ask whether the operator is open to discussing territory expansion, second-concept growth, or franchise ownership in Houston.
