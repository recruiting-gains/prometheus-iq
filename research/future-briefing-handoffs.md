# Research Briefing Future Handoffs

This document prepares future handoffs. It does not authorize implementation.

## Companion Handoff

Future task:

Allow Companion to summarize approved founder-safe Research briefings.

Research must provide:

- Brief ID.
- Briefing type.
- Reporting window.
- Executive summary.
- Verified facts.
- Hypotheses.
- Founder action recommendations.
- Source references.
- Uncertainty and limitations.

Companion must not:

- Convert hypotheses into facts.
- Write memory from unapproved drafts.
- Expose private customer data.
- Change Dashboard, CRM, Website, Infrastructure, or Intelligence Engine behavior.

Acceptance conditions:

- Brief status is approved for Companion handoff.
- Source references are present.
- Founder-safe summary excludes confidential sources unless explicitly approved.

## Intelligence Engine Handoff

Future task:

Allow approved Research briefings to become contextual evidence for the Adaptive Outside Sales Intelligence Engine.

Research must provide:

- Source tier.
- Citation.
- Truth level.
- Confidence.
- Category.
- Industry lens.
- Strategic implication.
- Limitation.

Intelligence Engine must not:

- Treat Research as direct route proof.
- Change scoring from Research input alone.
- Admit unsupported or uncited claims.
- Consume draft briefings as production evidence.

Acceptance conditions:

- Research source policy passes.
- Briefing schema validates.
- Intelligence contract admission explicitly accepts Research context.
- Evidence is contextual until promoted by Intelligence review.

## Website Handoff

Future task:

Allow selected Research insights to become public education or market-positioning content.

Research must provide:

- Public-safe insight candidate.
- Source references.
- Claims that can be externally supported.
- Internal-only exclusions.
- Risk notes.

Website must not:

- Publish internal strategy.
- Publish unsupported market claims.
- Publish confidential customer, beta, or founder operational details.
- Imply production capabilities that do not exist.

Acceptance conditions:

- Insight is marked public-safe.
- Brand and marketing approval is recorded.
- Legal/compliance review is requested when claims are sensitive.

## Sales Handoff

Future task:

Allow Sales to reuse Research intelligence for discovery, objections, industry positioning, and customer education.

Research must provide:

- Customer pain point findings.
- Competitive positioning updates.
- Industry-specific opportunity notes.
- Risk and objection signals.
- Source references and limitations.

Sales must not:

- Treat Research hypotheses as customer-facing facts.
- Claim production capabilities before implementation.
- Override Sales methodology or CRM lifecycle records.

Acceptance conditions:

- Sales accepts the handoff.
- Customer-facing language separates facts from hypotheses.
- Source limitations are preserved.
