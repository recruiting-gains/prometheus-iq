# Executive Intelligence Platform v1

Owner: Prometheus Research

Status: Active Research standard

Production runtime change: false

## Mission

Prometheus Research no longer treats its highest-value output as a sales report or dossier. Research produces Executive Intelligence Packages.

Research prepares executive decision-makers for high-impact business conversations by reducing uncertainty through evidence.

Minimum operating question:

What would a Fortune 100 Corporate Development team want to know before making an important strategic decision involving this business?

If a material answer is still missing, the package must either continue improving or explicitly mark the answer as unknown with verification priority.

## Research Role

Research does not gather information as an end state.

Research builds decision readiness.

Research prepares executives.

Dashboard displays.

CRM tracks.

Intelligence interprets.

Overwatch governs.

## Executive Intelligence Package Definition

An Executive Intelligence Package is a complete operational intelligence briefing that contains every lawful piece of information that materially improves executive decision quality.

Every package must:

- Preserve public-source attribution.
- Separate observed facts, corroborated facts, owner-provided facts, unknowns, and inferences.
- Identify source reliability.
- Identify confidence.
- Identify verification needed.
- Identify business decision impact.
- Identify conversation implications.
- Preserve unknowns as decision intelligence.
- Avoid private information, psychological profiling, hidden records, surveillance, restricted-platform scraping, and production-system mutation.

## Intelligence Layers

Every Executive Intelligence Package must cover these layers:

1. Executive Summary.
2. Business Identity.
3. Ownership, Leadership, Public Operators, Corporate Structure, Timeline.
4. Business Evolution, Expansion, Growth, Brand Development, Historical Milestones.
5. Operational Intelligence: Locations, Footprint, Hiring, Business Model, Operational Maturity, Growth Indicators, Expansion Signals.
6. Commercial Ecosystem Intelligence: Adjacent businesses, Anchor businesses, Traffic generators, Medical, Fitness, Retail, Residential, Commercial development, Economic activity, Future development, Infrastructure, Road projects, Construction, Population trends.
7. Competitive Intelligence: Direct competitors, Indirect competitors, Substitutes, Complementary businesses, Market positioning, Competitive strengths, Competitive weaknesses.
8. Community Intelligence: Community involvement, Partnerships, Organizations, Events, Networking, Brand presence, Public reputation.
9. Digital Intelligence: Website, SEO, Social presence, Marketing maturity, Digital footprint, Customer journey.
10. Reputation Intelligence: Review analysis, Theme analysis, Operational strengths, Operational weaknesses, Complaint patterns, Recovery behavior.
11. Strategic Intelligence: Growth probability, Expansion readiness, Business maturity, Investment readiness, Operational complexity, Decision-maker readiness.
12. Executive Decision Support: Top Opportunities, Top Risks, Critical Unknowns, Questions that reduce uncertainty, Conversation strategy, Recommended executive approach, Recommended next investigation.

## Evidence Labels

Every conclusion must use exactly one label:

- Observed: directly visible in a lawful public source.
- Corroborated: confirmed by more than one lawful public source.
- Owner-provided: provided directly by an authorized business representative.
- Unknown: not established by available lawful or owner-provided evidence.
- Inference: reasoned conclusion derived from labeled evidence.

No unlabeled conclusion is allowed in an Executive Intelligence Package.

## Required Conclusion Fields

Every recommendation and major conclusion must include:

- Evidence basis.
- Source reliability.
- Confidence level.
- Verification needed.
- Business decision impact.

## Quality Standard

Research must continuously ask:

What would a Fortune 100 Corporate Development team still want answered?

If the answer is material, the package is incomplete unless the gap is explicitly logged as an unknown with verification priority.

- Credit reports.
- Bank records.
- Tax records.
- Hidden records.
- Surveillance.
- Psychological profiling.
- Personal dossiers.
- Unattributed claims.
- Dashboard, CRM, Website, Infrastructure, Companion, Mobile, Fitness Connection, Route Intelligence, Active Pool, or Intelligence runtime mutations.

## Package Lifecycle

1. Candidate selection.
2. Lawful source inventory.
3. Evidence capture.
4. Source reliability scoring.
5. Intelligence layer completion.
6. Unknown register creation.
7. Red-team review.
8. Executive decision-support synthesis.
9. Package quality gate.
10. Canonical Research package registration.
11. Optional future read-only handoff to Dashboard, CRM, Intelligence, Sales, or Companion.

## Legacy Dossier Transition

Existing Executive Due Diligence Dossiers remain historical Research artifacts and current Dashboard inputs until a future approved Dashboard handoff migrates consumers to Executive Intelligence Packages.

New Research work must not create new dossier artifacts as the primary output. New work must create Executive Intelligence Packages and package registers.
