# Prometheus IQ Pilot Success Metrics Framework

Primary source document:

`docs/prometheus-iq-pilot-success-metrics-framework.md`

Use this framework to evaluate a 14-day Fitness Connection pilot with 3 approved users.

## Executive Purpose

Determine whether Prometheus IQ improved field-sales visibility, accountability, route usefulness, and measurable LSM outcomes.

## Metrics To Track

- Locations visited.
- GPS verified visits.
- LSM events attended.
- Leads generated.
- Membership units sold.
- Conversion rate.
- Follow-ups completed.
- Time spent in field.
- Productivity per field session.

## Success Levels

- Minimum Success: enough evidence to justify further controlled testing.
- Strong Success: clear operational value and larger pilot consideration.
- Exceptional Success: strong business case for executive expansion discussion.

## Do Not Send Yet

Do not send with real results until the pilot is completed and numbers are verified.
