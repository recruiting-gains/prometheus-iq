# Prometheus Research Foundation

Canonical source: `research/README.md` and `research/architecture.md`.

This file exists as the root entrypoint for future engineering prompts that reference `RESEARCH_FOUNDATION.md`. It does not replace the Research subsystem files.

Research is Prometheus' strategic intelligence division. It produces verified intelligence for founders, engineering, product strategy, sales, marketing, and future AI decision making.

Research must not modify production data or directly modify Dashboard, Companion, CRM, Infrastructure, Website, or Intelligence Engine behavior.
