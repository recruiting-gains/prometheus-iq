# Prometheus Product Vision v1

Purpose: define the canonical product direction for the Prometheus.IQ platform. This document is the single source of truth for product vision across Prometheus.IQ, Prometheus Route IQ, Companion, and future products. `COMPANY_IDENTITY.md` remains the canonical company identity source.

## 1. Mission

Prometheus exists to make outside sales intelligent, directed, accountable, and compounding.

Prometheus helps organizations that sell in the physical world know where to go, why to go there, what happened, what was learned, and what should happen next. The product mission is to convert field activity into an intelligent operating loop that improves routes, territories, coaching, follow-up, opportunity discovery, and executive visibility over time.

## 2. Long-Term Vision

Prometheus should become the AI operating system for organizations that rely on outside sales professionals.

Over the next 5 to 10 years, Prometheus should become the intelligence layer behind every field-sales motion: daily planning, territory learning, visit verification, route prioritization, opportunity discovery, manager coaching, performance insight, customer expansion, and executive operating visibility.

Prometheus should not merely record outside sales after the fact. It should help teams make better decisions before, during, and after field work.

## 3. Company Hierarchy

- Prometheus.IQ is the company.
- Prometheus.IQ owns the long-term outside-sales intelligence category.
- Prometheus Route IQ is the flagship product.
- Companion is the founder executive operating system.
- Gym and fitness sales are the first production validation environment only, not the company category.

Company identity decisions remain governed by `COMPANY_IDENTITY.md`. This product vision translates that identity into product direction, product hierarchy, customer hierarchy, roadmap priorities, and decision rules.

## 4. Product Hierarchy

### Prometheus.IQ

Prometheus.IQ is the company and ecosystem. It owns the operating philosophy, product portfolio, customer trust, data discipline, and long-term market position around outside-sales intelligence.

### Prometheus Route IQ

Prometheus Route IQ is the flagship product: a governed field-decision intelligence system for organizations that rely on outside sales professionals across industries.

Its purpose is to help sales organizations plan field work, prioritize routes and accounts, learn from visits, verify intentional activity without surveillance, improve team performance, and give leadership a clearer view of field reality.

### Companion

Companion is the founder executive operating system. It exists to support founder memory, strategy, continuity, operational reflection, and executive decision-making.

Companion is not the public product, not a customer-facing assistant, and not a runtime dependency for Prometheus Route IQ.

### Future Products

Future products may extend Prometheus into adjacent field operations, customer success, vertical playbooks, executive intelligence, or enterprise operating layers. Any future product must strengthen the outside-sales intelligence ecosystem rather than dilute it into a generic AI suite.

## 5. Customer Hierarchy

Prometheus serves organizations where revenue depends on outside sales activity in the physical world.

Primary customers:

- Outside sales representatives who need clear daily direction.
- Sales managers who need field visibility, coaching leverage, and consistent execution.
- Regional and district leaders who need territory performance and operating insight.
- Business owners who need growth without wasteful field motion.
- Enterprise organizations that need governed, secure, scalable outside-sales intelligence.

Validation customer:

- Gym and fitness sales are the first production validation environment because they provide real-world route pressure, local prospecting, relationship selling, measurable outcomes, and repeated field motion.

Gym sales validates the operating loop. It does not define the market.

## 6. Product Principles

- AI assists; humans decide.
- Explainable intelligence over mysterious automation.
- Field reality over dashboard theory.
- Mobile-first for field workflows.
- Enterprise-ready from the foundation.
- Fast enough for daily field use.
- Reliable enough to become operating infrastructure.
- Minimal friction for representatives.
- Secure by design.
- Proof without surveillance.
- Build once, reuse everywhere.
- Intelligence before activity.
- Every feature must reduce waste, increase clarity, improve outcomes, or compound learning.

## 7. Outside Sales Focus

Prometheus is built for outside sales professionals and the organizations that depend on them.

The core product question is:

Where should the team go next, why, and what did the field teach us?

Prometheus should help answer:

- Which accounts, locations, neighborhoods, or territories deserve attention?
- Which routes waste time?
- Which visit patterns create pipeline, revenue, or learning?
- Which representatives need coaching, support, or better territory design?
- Which field signals should leadership trust?
- Which actions should happen next?

Prometheus should not optimize for generic sales administration. It should optimize for field execution, field learning, and field accountability.

## 8. Industry Expansion Strategy

Prometheus begins in gym and fitness sales for production validation, then expands horizontally across outside-sales industries.

Priority expansion categories include:

- Healthcare.
- Medical devices.
- Solar.
- Roofing.
- HVAC.
- Security.
- Commercial cleaning.
- Telecommunications.
- Payroll.
- Insurance.
- Industrial sales.
- Manufacturing.
- Logistics.
- Construction.
- Local services.

Expansion should follow repeatable outside-sales patterns, not vertical novelty. Prometheus should adapt language, metrics, and workflows by industry while preserving the same core intelligence loop.

## 9. Enterprise Strategy

Prometheus must be enterprise-ready without becoming enterprise-bloated.

Enterprise strategy requires:

- Clear product boundaries.
- Security and data governance.
- Explainable recommendations.
- Admin and permission models.
- Auditability.
- Integration readiness.
- Multi-rep, multi-manager, multi-location, and multi-region support.
- Measurable ROI.
- Procurement, legal, and executive trust.

Enterprise adoption should be earned through operational value, not feature volume.

## 10. AI Strategy

Prometheus uses AI to improve human field decisions.

AI should:

- Recommend where to go.
- Explain why a recommendation exists.
- Learn from verified outcomes.
- Surface patterns humans would miss.
- Reduce administrative work.
- Support coaching.
- Improve forecasting and territory strategy.
- Preserve human override.

AI should not:

- Replace human judgment.
- Pretend weak evidence is certainty.
- Hide recommendation logic from operators.
- Become a generic chatbot layer.
- Create surveillance pressure.
- Optimize for novelty over usefulness.

## 11. Intelligence Strategy

Prometheus intelligence should compound through evidence.

The intelligence layer should learn from:

- Visit outcomes.
- Route behavior.
- Territory results.
- Time windows.
- Location categories.
- Permission and access patterns.
- Follow-up outcomes.
- Rep strengths and weaknesses.
- Account and organization performance.
- Industry context.
- Human overrides.

Every intelligence output should preserve evidence, confidence, explanation, and allowed influence. Weak evidence should produce cautious recommendations. Strong evidence should produce clearer operating guidance.

## 13. Competitive Positioning

Prometheus competes with:

- CRMs.
- Route planners.
- Sales engagement tools.
- BI dashboards.
- Generic AI assistants.
- Field activity trackers.
- Spreadsheets and manual planning.
- Internal operations processes.

Prometheus is different because it helps outside sales teams decide where to create opportunities before those opportunities exist in a CRM.

Customers choose Prometheus because it combines field planning, route intelligence, production tracking, explainable AI, manager visibility, and compounding territory learning.

Prometheus is difficult to copy because defensibility comes from lived field insight, workflow depth, accumulated evidence, trust, operating discipline, customer-specific learning, and the ability to expand across industries without losing the outside-sales core.

## 14. Founder Philosophy

Prometheus is founder-led from field reality.

The founder philosophy is:

- Build from lived outside-sales pain.
- Trust direct observation.
- Prefer useful systems over impressive demos.
- Preserve long-term company identity.
- Challenge category drift early.
- Protect customers from unnecessary complexity.
- Treat AI as leverage, not identity.
- Build enterprise trust before enterprise pressure forces it.

Founder-led does not mean founder-dependent. Prometheus must preserve decisions, context, and operating principles so the company can scale beyond one person's memory.

## 15. Decision Framework

Every major product decision should be tested against these questions:

1. Does this strengthen Prometheus as the AI operating system for outside sales?
2. Does this help customers decide, execute, learn, coach, or govern field work better?
3. Does this preserve human judgment?
4. Does this improve explainability, trust, speed, or reliability?
5. Does this compound intelligence rather than create disconnected features?
6. Does this apply beyond the first validation environment?
7. Does this avoid becoming CRM clutter, surveillance, or generic AI wrapping?
8. Does this reuse existing Prometheus foundations before creating new systems?
9. Can this become enterprise-safe over time?
10. Would this decision still make sense five years from now?

Ideas that fail these questions should be challenged, narrowed, deferred, or rejected.
