# Figure 5 - Customer Learning Workflow

## Figure Title

Customer Learning Workflow

## Purpose

Show how Route IQ updates customer intelligence from interactions, observed attributes, qualification evidence, objections, readiness signals, and follow-up requirements.

## Components

- Customer record input.
- Representative interaction input.
- Qualification evaluator.
- Objection and signal classifier.
- Relationship stage evaluator.
- Evidence completeness evaluator.
- Follow-up requirement evaluator.
- Customer intelligence updater.
- Customer intelligence data store.
- Probability engine input.

## Relationships

- Customer records and representative interactions provide the input evidence.
- Qualification, objection, readiness, relationship, evidence completeness, and follow-up evaluators update customer attributes.
- Customer intelligence updater writes revised customer attributes to the customer intelligence data store.
- Updated customer intelligence feeds probability calculations and representative recommendations.

## Narrative Description

Figure 5 depicts customer learning as a workflow that updates customer-specific intelligence after each interaction or data update. Route IQ receives a customer record and evidence from representative activity, such as notes, contact outcome, customer category, observed fit, objection, buying signal, disqualification reason, or follow-up commitment. The system evaluates whether the customer is qualified, whether the customer shows readiness signals, whether objections have changed, whether evidence is complete, and whether a follow-up is required. The customer intelligence updater stores updated customer attributes. The probability and recommendation engines use those updated attributes to decide future customer priority, visit objectives, follow-up timing, and evidence prompts.
