# Figure 6 - Outcome Learning Workflow

## Figure Title

Outcome Learning Workflow

## Purpose

Show how Route IQ learns from completed actions by comparing recommendations, representative behavior, predicted probabilities, and actual outcomes.

## Components

- Prior recommendation record.
- Predicted probability record.
- Representative action record.
- Actual outcome record.
- Outcome classifier.
- Prediction comparison engine.
- Weight or rule adjustment module.
- Outcome intelligence updater.
- Outcome intelligence data store.
- Probability engine feedback input.

## Relationships

- Prior recommendations and predicted probabilities are compared with representative actions and actual outcomes.
- Outcome classifier categorizes the result.
- Prediction comparison engine determines whether the result matched, contradicted, or failed to resolve the prior prediction.
- Weight or rule adjustment module updates internal scoring or decision parameters where applicable.
- Outcome intelligence updater stores the resulting outcome intelligence.
- Updated outcome intelligence feeds future probability calculations.

## Narrative Description

Figure 6 depicts outcome learning after a representative action is completed or otherwise resolved. Route IQ retrieves the prior recommendation and predicted probability associated with the action. The system also receives the representative action record and actual outcome, such as successful contact, no contact, disqualification, follow-up creation, demo scheduling, sale progression, lost opportunity, or incomplete evidence. An outcome classifier categorizes the result. A prediction comparison engine compares the predicted probability to the actual result. The system may adjust weights, rules, confidence values, or future recommendation factors and stores the result in outcome intelligence. Future probability calculations use the updated outcome intelligence.
