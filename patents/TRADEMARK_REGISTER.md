# Trademark Register

Initialized: 2026-07-09

Purpose: Track Prometheus brand names, product names, slogans, logos, and usage evidence for attorney review.

This register does not file trademark applications and does not determine registrability or clearance.

## Register Fields

Each record should include:

- Identifier.
- Mark.
- Type.
- Goods or services context.
- First known use.
- Public use evidence.
- Logo or design references.
- Related domain or social handles.
- Clearance questions.
- Attorney-review questions.
