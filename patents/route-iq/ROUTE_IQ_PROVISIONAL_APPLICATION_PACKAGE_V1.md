Founder-confirmed timeline:

| Date | Event | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May 1, 2026 | First conception of Route IQ. | Founder-confirmed. |
| May 3, 2026 | Decision to create the Representative Intelligence Loop. | Founder-confirmed. |
| May 8, 2026 | First working implementation, represented by ART-003. | Founder-confirmed. |

Founder-confirmed invention history summary:

- The Founder identified that field representatives were being sent into territories without meaningful intelligence and were relying primarily on Google Maps to decide where to go next.
- HubSpot CRM stored customer information but did not continuously learn the representative or generate adaptive visit recommendations.
- The Founder observed that representatives often performed well in certain locations but poorly in others.
- The key technical insight was to continuously learn the representative, territory, customer, and outcomes through an adaptive intelligence loop that improves future recommendations.
- The Founder conceived the invention independently and reported no early invention discussions.
