# Route IQ USPTO Provisional Filing Workspace v1

Initialized: 2026-07-09

Invention: IP-001 - Route IQ Adaptive Representative Intelligence System

Purpose: Prepare a Founder-facing workspace for manual entry of Route IQ provisional application information into the USPTO filing system.

Boundary: This workspace does not submit anything to the USPTO, draft patent claims, complete USPTO forms, make legal representations, provide legal analysis, or determine patentability. Founder-specific legal and personal fields are intentionally blank unless already present in the patent preparation record.

Official references for Founder verification:

- USPTO provisional application page: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/apply/provisional-application
- USPTO patent application forms page: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/apply/forms
- USPTO fee schedule: https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/fees-and-payment/uspto-fee-schedule
- USPTO Patent Center: https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/

## Status Legend

- Already completed: The patent preparation record contains the value or attachment reference.
- Requires Founder input: The field needs Founder-specific information or a Founder decision.
- Requires attorney confirmation: The field should be confirmed by patent counsel before relying on it for filing.
- Requires USPTO portal entry: The Founder must enter or upload the item manually in the USPTO filing workflow.
