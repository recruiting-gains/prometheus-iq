## 1. First Conception Date

Status: Founder-confirmed

Current record:

- Founder-confirmed first conception date: May 1, 2026.
- Founder-confirmed problem that led to the invention: Field representatives were being sent into territories without meaningful intelligence, resulting in unnecessary driving, wasted fuel, and reliance primarily on Google Maps to decide where to go next.
- Founder-confirmed existing product limitation: HubSpot CRM stored customer information but did not solve the decision of where a specific representative should go next, did not continuously learn the representative, and did not generate adaptive visit recommendations.
- Founder-confirmed differentiation: The Founder observed that representatives often performed well in certain locations but poorly in others, and that simply sending representatives to different businesses did not improve outcomes.
- Founder-confirmed key insight: The system should continuously learn the representative, not just the territory or customer, and adapt recommendations based on that representative's historical performance.

Repository evidence currently available:

- `docs/prometheus-multi-rep-intelligence-plan.md` is dated June 22, 2026 and documents separate representative intelligence profiles, founder baseline preservation, Anthony new representative mode, independent visit history, independent route history, recommendation history, performance metrics, and learning history.
- Git commit `e9d3b46` dated 2026-06-22, `Add Anthony prospect seed routing`, includes `docs/prometheus-multi-rep-intelligence-plan.md`, `index.html`, deployed dashboard HTML, qualified prospect data, and route-building scripts.

## 15. Continuous Adaptive Route Replenishment v1

Status: Engineering expansion documented before implementation

Recorded: 2026-07-11

Supporting record:

- `patents/route-iq/CONTINUOUS_ADAPTIVE_ROUTE_REPLENISHMENT_V1.md`

Technical relationship:

- Extends outcome-driven recommendation regeneration into continuous, bounded route queue maintenance.
- Applies the latest available representative, territory, business, category, time-window, follow-up, objection, closing, and historical outcome evidence before selecting a replacement.
- Maintains up to 15 active recommendations, appends replacements without rebuilding the route, and preserves completed-stop history.
- Uses Active Pool candidates first and the governed Full Pool only as fallback.
- Fails closed for unknown workspaces and prevents duplicate, completed, quarantined, or cross-workspace recommendations.
- Uses a single-flight lock and bounded candidate scans to prevent overlapping replenishment and infinite generation loops.

Evidence classification:

- Preserve this record under the existing IP-001 evidence history.
- Preserve scoring weights and proprietary ranking details as confidential implementation information.

## 19. Shadow Decision Validation and Recommendation Readiness Gate v1

Status: Post-provisional material invention candidate documented before implementation

Batch ID: BATCH-20260713-001

Recorded: 2026-07-13

Date conceived: 2026-07-13

Supporting record:

- `patents/route-iq/SHADOW_DECISION_VALIDATION_AND_RECOMMENDATION_READINESS_GATE_V1.md`

Technical relationship:

- Extends the Route IQ evidence and learning pipeline with zero-influence prospective validation before recommendation influence is considered.
- Freezes evidence at each eligible decision timestamp and rejects later-available evidence from the original prediction.
- Records one deterministic, versioned shadow decision or one documented exclusion for every eligible opportunity.
- Preserves ranked candidate decisions, probabilities, confidence, evidence cutoff, evidence and schema versions, model or rule version, and eligibility or exclusion reasons.
- Links later representative actions and observed outcomes through append-only evaluation records without rewriting the original shadow decision.
- Uses chronological, non-overlapping evaluation evidence and a declared observational baseline.
- Evaluates coverage, outcome linkage, calibration, accuracy, stability, and baseline-relative performance by representative, territory, customer context, decision type, and time window.
- Uses documented statistical reasoning and segment-safety checks before producing `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `OBSERVING`, or `ELIGIBLE_FOR_FOUNDER_REVIEW`.
- Keeps route ordering, Discovery Mode, Nearby Context, Saved Places, notifications, representative interfaces, operational workflows, automatic actions, and recommendation influence unchanged.
