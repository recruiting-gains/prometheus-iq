# Trade Secret Register

Initialized: 2026-07-09

Purpose: Track Prometheus trade secret-support materials and confidentiality-sensitive technical information for attorney review.

This register does not make legal conclusions about trade secret status.

## Register Fields

Each record should include:

- Identifier.
- Working title.
- Description.
- Business value.
- Confidentiality status.
- Access controls.
- Storage location.
- Disclosure history.
- Relationship to patent, copyright, or trademark materials.
- Attorney-review questions.

## Initial Candidate Categories

### TS-001 - Prospect Qualification Rubrics

Status: Category scaffold

Description: Internal criteria, scoring, segmentation, and promotion rules used to qualify prospects and runtime pools.

Confidentiality note: Treat detailed rules and thresholds as confidential until counsel and company leadership decide otherwise.

### TS-002 - Evidence Weighting and Confidence Methods

Status: Category scaffold

Description: Internal methods for weighing evidence, interpreting source reliability, and converting evidence into recommendations.

Confidentiality note: Treat internal weighting methods as confidential unless intentionally disclosed.

### TS-003 - Customer and Market Intelligence Workflows

Status: Category scaffold

Description: Internal workflows for collecting, organizing, and activating customer, competitor, and market intelligence.

Confidentiality note: Treat customer-specific intelligence and operational playbooks as confidential.

### TS-004 - Founder Operating and Decision Systems

Status: Category scaffold

Description: Internal operating-system methods for decision tracking, handoffs, continuity, and execution prioritization.

Confidentiality note: Treat internal decision frameworks and operating cadence as confidential unless published.

## Handling Rules

- Do not publish detailed trade secret candidates without leadership and counsel review.
- Record any external disclosure.
- Keep source paths and sensitive business rules limited to appropriate internal repositories.
- Separate attorney-review summaries from public marketing language.

## Intake Backlog

- Identify specific files and formulas that may need confidential handling.
- Record current access controls.
- Record any public disclosures.
- Prepare attorney-review confidentiality questions.
