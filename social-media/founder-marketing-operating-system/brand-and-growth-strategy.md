# Brand Consistency And Founder Growth Strategy v1

Owner: Prometheus Social Media

## Brand Position

Prometheus is the AI operating system for outside sales.

Prometheus Route IQ is the flagship product and first expression of the larger Prometheus.IQ company vision.

Gym sales is the first validation environment, not the market category.

## Core Messaging

Use:

- Outside sales intelligence.
- Field reality.
- Better-directed motion.
- Proof without surveillance.
- Compounding learning.
- Sales teams knowing where to go next.

Avoid:

- Generic CRM clone language.
- Surveillance framing.
- Gym-only positioning.
- Route planner as the full category.
- Unverified performance claims.
- Claims that live analytics or integrations are connected before they are.

## Voice

Prometheus founder voice should be:

- Direct.
- Practical.
- Field-informed.
- Ambitious.
- Specific.
- Enterprise-trustworthy.

It should not be:

- Hype-driven.
- Trend-chasing.
- Overly casual for enterprise buyers.
- Secretive about outcomes.
- Loose with private operational details.

## Founder Growth Strategy

Phase 1: Credibility

- Publish founder lessons consistently.
- Teach the outside-sales problem.
- Show disciplined company building.
- Build trust through clarity.

Phase 2: Category

- Name the gap between CRM and field action.
- Explain why outside sales needs intelligence before activity.
- Build a repeatable set of terms and frameworks.

Phase 3: Demand

- Convert audience attention into pilot interest.
- Route interested conversations to Sales or CRM only after those systems request implementation.
- Keep all attribution manual until Website and CRM contracts are implemented.

Phase 4: Authority

- Turn validated posts into founder essays, video explainers, sales collateral, and investor-ready positioning.

## Reuse System

Every strong post should be reviewed for reuse:

- LinkedIn essay.
- YouTube short.
- Sales objection answer.
- Website FAQ candidate.
- Investor narrative proof point.
- Founder talk track.

Website and Sales reuse requires subsystem handoff. Social Media does not modify those assets here.

## Brand Consistency Checklist

- Prometheus is positioned as broad outside-sales infrastructure.
- Prometheus Route IQ is named correctly as the flagship product.
- Field reality is central.
- Proprietary intelligence is protected.
- Claims are verifiable.
- Beta/customer details are protected.
- CTA is appropriate for current subsystem readiness.
