# Founder Content Workflow v1

Owner: Prometheus Social Media

## Inputs

Founder content should come from verified sources:

- Company identity.
- Product vision.
- Field-sales lessons.
- Founder decisions.
- Marketing campaign goals.
- Approved public website positioning.
- Approved sales language.

Do not use:

- Private customer data.
- Anthony beta details.
- Unverified performance claims.
- Proprietary recommendation logic.
- Live analytics that are not connected.

## Workflow

1. Capture

Record the raw idea in one sentence:

```text
Field reality:
Founder lesson:
Audience:
Why this matters:
```

2. Classify

Choose one content pillar:

- Field Reality.
- Product Intelligence.
- Founder Build In Public.
- Enterprise Trust.
- Outside Sales Education.

3. Draft

Use the platform-specific format:

- LinkedIn: structured founder insight.
- Instagram: visual or short caption.
- X: direct short-form observation.
- YouTube: teaching or founder narrative.
- TikTok: high-context short-form hook.

4. Review

Check:

- Does this say what Prometheus does without exposing how proprietary intelligence works?
- Does this position Prometheus beyond gym sales?
- Does this avoid surveillance language?
- Does this avoid Anthony beta specifics?
- Is the CTA manual and safe?

5. Publish Manually

Publish directly in the platform UI by a human account owner. No automated posting is part of v1.

6. Capture Metrics

Until live providers are connected, record manual metrics only:

- Platform.
- Post URL.
- Date.
- Campaign.
- Impressions if visible.
- Reach if visible.
- Views if visible.
- Likes.
- Comments.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Follows.
- Website clicks if visible.
- Qualitative replies.

7. Learn

Classify each post:

- Reuse.
- Improve.
- Retire.

The decision should be based on audience signal, not vanity metrics alone.

## Review Checklist

- Brand aligned.
- Clear outside-sales category.
- No confidential data.
- No API or integration claims.
- No live metric claims unless verified.
- No beta customer exposure.
- Manual publishing only.
