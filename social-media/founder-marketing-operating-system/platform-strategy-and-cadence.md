# Platform Strategy And Cadence v1

Owner: Prometheus Social Media

## Weekly Cadence

Minimum operating cadence:

- LinkedIn: 3 posts per week.
- Instagram: 3 posts or reels per week plus optional stories.
- X: 5 short posts per week.
- YouTube: 1 short per week, 1 long-form or founder explainer per month.
- TikTok: 2 short-form concepts per week when founder capacity allows.

Do not treat this as an automation schedule. All v1 publishing is manual.

## Instagram

Role:

- Founder presence.
- Build-in-public moments.
- Simple product education.
- Visual proof of company momentum.

Best formats:

- Short founder clips.
- Carousel lessons.
- Field-sales observations.
- Screenshot-free product concepts.

Do not:

- Use live Instagram API metrics unless connected later through Social Media provider controls.
- Auto-post.
- Scrape comments.
- Store passwords.

## LinkedIn

Role:

- Enterprise credibility.
- Outside-sales category education.
- Founder authority.
- Investor and customer trust.

Best formats:

- Founder essays.
- Field-sales frameworks.
- Enterprise problem posts.
- Product vision posts.

Primary audience:

- Sales leaders.
- Operators.
- Founders.
- Investors.
- Field-sales managers.

## X

Role:

- Fast idea testing.
- Founder voice.
- Short market observations.
- Public learning loop.

Best formats:

- Direct one-liners.
- Short threads.
- Contrarian sales observations.
- Build notes.

Use X to test hooks before expanding them into LinkedIn or YouTube.

## YouTube

Role:

- Durable education.
- Founder narrative.
- Product category explanation.
- Searchable proof of thinking.

Best formats:

- 60-second shorts.
- 5 to 8 minute founder explainers.
- Outside-sales problem breakdowns.
- Product philosophy videos.

YouTube should preserve high-quality explanations, not daily noise.

## TikTok

Role:

- Hook testing.
- Founder relatability.
- High-energy outside-sales education.

Best formats:

- Problem hooks.
- Field-sales myth busting.
- Founder lessons.
- Simple visual metaphors.

Do not chase trends that weaken enterprise trust.

## Cross-Platform Reuse

One founder insight should become:

- LinkedIn post.
- X short post.
- Instagram caption or carousel.
- TikTok hook.
- YouTube short.

Do not copy identical text everywhere. Adapt the idea to the platform.
