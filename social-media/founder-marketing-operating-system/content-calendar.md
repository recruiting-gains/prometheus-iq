# Content Calendar v1

Owner: Prometheus Social Media

## Calendar Model

The Prometheus calendar uses four reusable weekly themes. Repeat the cycle monthly and update examples from current founder work.

## Weekly Themes

Week 1: Field Reality

- Core idea: outside sales is won in physical conditions, not spreadsheets.
- Proof inputs: founder field notes, wasted-route examples, rep pain, missed follow-up, local territory learning.
- Primary platforms: LinkedIn, Instagram, X.
- CTA: follow Prometheus for outside-sales intelligence.

Week 2: Product Intelligence

- Core idea: Prometheus helps reps know where to go, why, and what to do next.
- Proof inputs: route logic, learning loops, territory decisions, dashboard concepts.
- Primary platforms: LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram.
- CTA: join beta interest or request pilot conversation when approved by Sales/Website.

Week 3: Founder Build In Public

- Core idea: the company is being built from real outside-sales experience.
- Proof inputs: founder decisions, company identity, product milestones, lessons learned.
- Primary platforms: Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok.
- CTA: follow the build.

Week 4: Enterprise Trust

- Core idea: Prometheus is an operating system for outside sales, not a route-planning gimmick.
- Proof inputs: security discipline, proof without surveillance, manager visibility, broad-market positioning.
- Primary platforms: LinkedIn, YouTube.
- CTA: save/share for outside-sales leaders.

## Monthly Content Inventory

Required monthly assets:

- 4 flagship LinkedIn posts.
- 8 short X posts.
- 8 Instagram posts or reels.
- 4 TikTok concepts.
- 2 YouTube short-form videos.
- 1 YouTube founder explainer.
- 1 monthly recap post.

## Daily Prompt Bank

Use one prompt per publish day:

- What did field sales teach us that a CRM cannot see?
- What does a rep need before walking into a territory?
- What gets mistaken for activity but does not create pipeline?
- What should a manager know without surveilling the rep?
- What did Prometheus learn from real route pressure this week?
- What makes outside sales different from inside sales?
- What would make tomorrow's route smarter than today's?

## Content States

Every content item should move through:

1. Idea.
2. Draft.
3. Founder review.
4. Manual publish.
5. Manual metric capture.
6. Learning note.
7. Reuse or retire.

Do not publish Anthony-specific beta details, screenshots, private route data, location logs, or performance claims unless Cruz explicitly approves customer-safe public language.
