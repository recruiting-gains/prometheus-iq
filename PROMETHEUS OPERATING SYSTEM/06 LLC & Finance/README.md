# 06 LLC & Finance

## Purpose

This folder is the business-formation and finance center. It keeps LLC, EIN, banking, bookkeeping, taxes, insurance, and financial controls from living only in the founder's head.

## Included Documents

- `docs/prometheus-llc-launch-plan.md`
- `docs/prometheus-pricing-strategy-playbook.md`

## When To Use Each Document

- LLC Launch Plan: Use before filing the Texas LLC, after approval, and during the first 90 days of company setup.
- Pricing Strategy Playbook: Use after pilot proof, before paid agreements, and when planning revenue.

## Who Should Use This Folder

- Founder
- CPA
- Bookkeeper
- Attorney
- CFO / finance lead
- Banking contact

## Future Documents That Belong Here

- Texas LLC filing documents.
- EIN confirmation.
- Operating agreement.
- Business bank account information checklist.
- Chart of accounts.
- Monthly bookkeeping close checklist.
- Startup expense tracker.
- Tax calendar.
- Insurance policy summary.
- W-9.
- Customer invoice template.

## Guardrail

Do not mix personal and business money after the business bank account is open. Do not take investment or promise equity without legal support.
