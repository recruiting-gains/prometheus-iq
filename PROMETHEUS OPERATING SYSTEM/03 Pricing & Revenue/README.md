# 03 Pricing & Revenue

## Purpose

This folder is the commercial strategy center for Prometheus. It helps the company price responsibly, avoid early-founder pricing mistakes, and convert pilots into durable revenue.

## Included Documents

- `docs/prometheus-pricing-strategy-playbook.md`
- `docs/competitor-pricing-revenue.md`

## When To Use Each Document

- Pricing Strategy Playbook: Use after a pilot succeeds or when leadership asks what a paid agreement could look like.
- Competitor Pricing Revenue Notes: Use internally to understand the surrounding market and avoid pricing in a vacuum.

## Who Should Use This Folder

- Founder
- CFO / finance support
- COO
- Sales lead
- Contract negotiator
- Future investor-relations support

## Future Documents That Belong Here

- Standard pricing sheet.
- Discount approval policy.
- Invoice template.
- Customer contract pricing appendix.
- Revenue forecast.
- Gross margin model.
- Renewal playbook.
- Expansion pricing calculator.

## Guardrail

Pricing should not be sent during initial pilot approval unless leadership directly asks. First earn trust, prove value, and then discuss paid structure.
