# Route IQ Shadow Decision Validation and Readiness Gate v1

This Intelligence-owned, Founder-only read path extends the governed field-session learning architecture and calls Data's `projectRouteIqChronologicalEvidence` at each eligible visit opportunity. It creates exactly one immutable shadow record or explicit exclusion per opportunity. It has no operational import, write, or control path; recommendation influence is OFF in every state.

The canonical July 11 archive contains four visits but no historically frozen prospective candidate list or recommendation context, so all four opportunities are excluded and readiness is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Validation-only fixtures are labeled non-production and are never inputs to the canonical generator.

Outcomes are append-only links. Missing, late, corrected, and conflicting observations remain separate and never rewrite a shadow decision. Equal-time evidence without proven prior availability and future evidence are excluded by the Data projection. Training and evaluation intervals are end-exclusive, chronological, non-overlapping, and overlap fails closed.

The prospective generator accepts an explicitly frozen opportunity containing candidate IDs, nonnegative weights, `availableAt`, evidence source IDs or SHA-256 hashes, and eligibility reasons. Every reference must resolve to a frozen immutable source manifest entry containing a canonical source ID, SHA-256, and `availableAt`. Candidate availability, manifest source availability, and every projected evidence timestamp must be strictly before the decision cutoff. The Data projection digest is recomputed from canonical projection content, and the candidate/source snapshot receives its own deterministic digest. Blank, unresolved, equal-time, future, mismatched, or malformed provenance fails closed.

The sample requirement uses the conservative proportion confidence-interval formula `ceil(z² p(1-p) / e²)`: at 95% confidence, p=.5, and margin .10, 97 linked evaluation decisions are required. Five declared calibration bins derive `ceil(97/5)=20` observations per bin. Two evaluation windows deterministically divide support as 49 and 48. A material segment is derived as `ceil(97*.10)=10` observations. These are design calculations, not unexplained counts.

Actual representative action is evaluated separately as decision agreement and may be off-list. Each binary outcome names its `evaluatedDecisionId`. The report separately publishes actual-action link rate, any-outcome link rate, and scoreable selected-candidate outcome rate. Outcome accuracy, Brier, ECE, baseline, stability, and segment metrics include an outcome only when that ID is the selected shadow candidate. Off-list and action-only links remain visible in every segment inventory but cannot be scored as the selected candidate's outcome. Missing or unresolved-conflict outcomes remain unavailable.

Five reliability bins report support, predicted mean, observed rate, and support status. ECE is available only when every populated bin meets its formula-derived support; no unsupported populated bin is silently dropped. Performance stability applies governed maximum deltas to outcome accuracy, Brier, and ECE across consecutive windows. Top-decision distribution total variation remains supplemental.

The baseline is the explicit selected-candidate binary outcome rate from a strictly prior training window and requires the same formula-derived 97-outcome support as evaluation. Every training outcome, including corrections, must be observed before the first evaluation window begins. Training and evaluation source IDs and hashes must also be disjoint. Baseline accuracy and Brier are evaluated on later windows, and deltas are observational comparisons only.

Representative, territory, customer-context, decision-type, and chronological-window segments are inventoried from all eligible shadow decisions. Each inventory reports action coverage, any-link coverage, scoreable outcome coverage, off-list actions, agreement, and outcome metrics separately. Unknown segments remain explicit. Any formula-material segment without sufficient selected-candidate outcomes or calibration support blocks eligibility even when aggregate metrics pass.

Every exclusion has a canonical timestamp and cutoff, authorized scope, nonempty reason codes, projection provenance, versions, deterministic ID, and deterministic hash. The shadow ledger hash covers immutable predictions and exclusions. Each append-only outcome link has its own digest, while outcome-chain and evaluated-ledger hashes change deterministically after every append. The Founder report publishes all evaluated hashes.

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` means opportunity, linked-outcome, baseline, or mathematical support cannot sustain evaluation. `OBSERVING` means sufficient evaluation exists but at least one coverage, baseline, calibration, stability, or segment gate has not passed. `ELIGIBLE_FOR_FOUNDER_REVIEW` requires complete declared action/outcome coverage, no unresolved evidence reasons, every exact gate, every material segment, and two consecutive chronological windows to pass. It never activates or authorizes recommendations.

Rollback removes or disables only this Intelligence consumer and its artifacts. No production rollback is needed because recommendation influence remains OFF.

The verifier requires the exact protected baseline key set and checks SHA-256 baselines for Dashboard source and deploy mirror, Worker, route generation, Discovery, Nearby, Active Pool, runtime pools, business hours, the established route-scoring implementation, and the route-quality gate. These files are never written by this subsystem.
