# Smoothie Depot Workspace Intelligence Plan v1

Status: planning contracts only.

Workspace: Smoothie Depot.

Decision maker: JR.

Operating Profile: Franchise Development.

Intelligence domain: Business Acquisition Intelligence inside the Prometheus Intelligence Engine.

This plan creates an isolated workspace intelligence plan for helping Cruz identify, qualify, track, and hand off potential Smoothie Depot franchise-owner candidates. It does not create a new subsystem, does not modify Dashboard runtime, does not modify CRM runtime, does not modify Website, does not modify Mobile, does not modify Companion, and does not alter Route Intelligence or production scoring.

## Workspace Boundary

Smoothie Depot owns its own workspace, candidate intelligence, JR handoffs, commission tracking, protection periods, and Business Acquisition Intelligence planning contracts.

Fitness Connection remains isolated. Fitness Connection data must not be read into Smoothie Depot candidate intelligence, and Smoothie Depot candidate intelligence must not influence Fitness Connection route recommendations.

Allowed shared layers:

- shared Intelligence Engine contracts
- shared Operating Profile architecture
- shared validation scripts
- shared anonymous architecture patterns

Blocked influence paths:

- Smoothie Depot candidate data -> Fitness Connection Route Intelligence
- Smoothie Depot candidate data -> Fitness Connection Active Pool
- Smoothie Depot candidate data -> Fitness Connection scoring
- Fitness Connection field data -> Smoothie Depot candidate ranking
- Smoothie Depot workspace data -> global production recommendations

## Planning Contracts

### 1. Franchise Candidate Sourcing

Purpose: identify potential franchise-owner candidates from workspace-approved sources.

Planned fields:

- candidate source
- source type
- candidate references
- research status
- generated by
- generated at
- isolation state

### 2. Business Prospect Research

Purpose: research each potential candidate's business background, ownership context, geography, and fit.

Planned fields:

- candidate reference
- business references
- research questions
- source references
- confidence
- review state

### 3. Franchise Readiness Assessment

Purpose: assess candidate readiness without automated scoring or production recommendation mutation.

Planned factors:

- capital signal
- owner/operator signal
- local-market fit
- franchise interest signal
- operating discipline
- follow-up readiness
- risk notes

### 4. Candidate Intelligence Profile

Purpose: create a reviewable candidate profile for Cruz and future JR handoff.

Planned fields:

- business background
- capital signals
- territory interest
- fit summary
- risks
- next action
- provenance

### 5. CRM Pipeline Stages

Purpose: plan CRM stages without implementing CRM runtime.

Planned stages:

- Candidate Sourced
- Researching
- Qualified
- JR Handoff
- Protected
- Commission Eligible
- Closed Won
- Closed Lost

### 6. Dashboard Display Needs

Purpose: plan future Dashboard display without Dashboard changes.

Planned display needs:

- candidate list
- readiness status
- source confidence
- protection status
- JR handoff status
- commission status
- next action
- evidence provenance

### 7. JR Handoff Package

Purpose: prepare a concise package for JR when Cruz is ready to hand off a candidate.

Planned sections:

- candidate summary
- why this candidate may fit
- evidence reviewed
- confidence
- risks
- recommended ask
- protection status
- commission status

### 8. Commission Tracking

Purpose: track commission eligibility planning without financial automation.

Planned fields:

- candidate reference
- trigger event
- estimated value
- status
- evidence references
- review state

### 9. Candidate Protection Period

Purpose: preserve the time window where Cruz's sourced candidate should remain protected.

Protection period dates remain configurable by agreement. This plan does not assign a legal duration.

### 10. Workspace Isolation Guardrails

Purpose: preserve hard separation between Smoothie Depot and Fitness Connection.

Guardrails:

- workspace ID required on every candidate artifact
- operating profile must be `franchise_development`
- intelligence domain must be `business_acquisition_intelligence`
- Fitness Connection records are prohibited
- route recommendation influence is prohibited
- Active Pool influence is prohibited
- Dashboard/CRM/Mobile/Website/Companion runtime activation is prohibited

## Future Handoffs

Future subsystem work may be handed to Dashboard or CRM only after Overwatch approves a runtime task. Until then, these planning contracts remain Intelligence-owned and workspace-scoped.
