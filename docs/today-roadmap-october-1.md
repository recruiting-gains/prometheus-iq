## App Upgrade

Approved direction:

- Visual style: command center.
- First feature: Route Intelligence.
- Tone: premium, tactical, serious, and built for real field sales.

Route Intelligence should help answer:

- What is the best next stop?
- Why is that stop recommended?
- What does today's activity warn me about?
- Am I logging enough visits and conversations?
- Is the route building useful data for tomorrow?

The app should not publicly explain secret scoring logic. It can show the result and practical reason without exposing the formula.

## Daily Prometheus Work Plan

Minimum daily work, no set times:

- Log real app data from every field-sales day.
- Improve one small app feature, screen, wording, or data point.
- Post Instagram Stories from the builder or field journey.
- Create one useful content piece: Reel, carousel, or field-sales lesson.
- Track interested people: DMs, comments, poll responses, beta requests.
- Review one competitor, customer problem, or VC question.
- End each day by writing one note: what worked, what failed, what Prometheus should learn next.

Weekly rhythm:

- 5 to 7 Reels.
- 35 to 70 Stories.
- 2 to 3 carousels.
- 1 landing page or pitch improvement.
- 1 app improvement review.
- 1 weekly metrics summary: units, visits, conversations, leads, routes, gas/miles if available, top category, best place.
