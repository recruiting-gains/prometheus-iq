# Prometheus Competitor Research

## Short Answer

Yes, companies are already building parts of the Prometheus idea.

The broad market exists:

- Field sales route planning
- Door-to-door canvassing apps
- Territory management
- Outside sales CRM
- Rep activity tracking
- Sales mapping
- AI lead scoring
- Manager dashboards
- Mileage/gas-saving route tools

Prometheus should not be positioned as if nobody has ever thought about route planning or field sales software. That would be inaccurate.

The better position is:

> Existing tools prove the market is real. Prometheus should win by focusing on a sharper, simpler, sales-result-driven wedge: helping local outside sales reps know where to go before the lead exists, starting with gym membership LSM.

## Closest Competitors

### Badger Maps

Badger Maps is a route planner and field sales assistant. It includes route planning, sales mapping, data collection, lead generation, mileage tracking, CRM integrations, territory mapping, reporting, analytics, and AI features.

Why it matters:

- Badger is very close to the route-planning and gas-saving side of Prometheus.
- It already positions itself around reducing driving and increasing sales.
- It integrates with HubSpot and Salesforce.

Prometheus difference:

- Prometheus can start much narrower and more practical for local membership sales.
- Prometheus can focus on daily LSM production, units per visit, and location-category learning instead of being a broad field-sales suite.

### SPOTIO

SPOTIO is a field sales execution platform with route optimization, territory management, activity tracking, lead management, performance analytics, pipeline management, and an AI co-pilot.

Why it matters:

- SPOTIO is probably one of the strongest direct competitors in the broader field-sales execution market.
- It directly says CRMs are not built for field sales.
- It positions itself as the field layer that integrates with CRM rather than replacing CRM.

Prometheus difference:

- Prometheus should not try to out-SPOTIO SPOTIO at first.
- Prometheus can win a smaller wedge: Fitness Connection LSM, gym field sales, and simple route intelligence tied to units.

### SalesRabbit

SalesRabbit is a field sales and canvassing platform with canvassing tools, territory mapping, lead management, DataGrid AI, buyer scores, industry-specific field sales workflows, and reporting/gamification.

Why it matters:

- SalesRabbit already serves door-to-door and high-volume field sales industries like roofing, solar, telecom, home security, pest control, and political/campaign work.
- It uses AI/data to help identify better targets.

Prometheus difference:

- Prometheus should avoid starting in heavily served roofing/solar/pest-control territory.
- Gym membership sales and local-store-marketing may be a more open first wedge.

### Map My Customers

Map My Customers calls itself a CRM for field reps and focuses on mapping, routing, lead finding, territory management, reporting, analytics, mileage tracking, heat maps, and field rep adoption.

Why it matters:

- It is close to the "outside rep CRM plus map" concept.
- It emphasizes mapping data into actionable routes and territory insights.

Prometheus difference:

- Prometheus can be less CRM-heavy and more "daily production engine."
- Prometheus can focus on route decisions and unit production before leads enter HubSpot.

### Outfield

Outfield is a mobile-first field sales platform that works standalone or alongside Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, or other CRMs. It includes route planning, account mapping, rep tracking, territory management, performance management, gamification, and pipeline management.

Why it matters:

- Outfield already says it is the mobile field layer for outside reps.
- It already speaks to HubSpot integration and route planning.

Prometheus difference:

- Prometheus should be lighter, faster, and more specialized.
- The first wedge should be a simple LSM sales command center, not a full sales CRM.

### Repsly

Repsly is more focused on retail execution, merchandising, field team management, in-store execution, image recognition, and CPG field teams.

Why it matters:

- Repsly proves field execution software is a real enterprise category.
- It is less directly door-to-door and more retail/CPG execution.

Prometheus difference:

- Prometheus should focus on outside prospecting and route intelligence, not shelf execution or merchandising.

## What This Means

Prometheus is not the only app in the general market.

The category is crowded enough that the pitch cannot be:

> Nobody is doing this.

The pitch should be:

> The market already proves companies pay for field-sales execution tools. Prometheus is a focused version built from real LSM field results, starting in gyms and expanding into other local outside-sales teams.

## Prometheus Wedge

The strongest wedge is:

> Prometheus helps outside reps decide where to create leads before those leads enter HubSpot or Salesforce.

Prometheus should be positioned around:

- Local-store-marketing sales teams.
- Gym membership sales.
- Daily unit goal execution.
- Route decisions from real field results.
- Gas/time savings.
- Simple rep adoption.
- Learning individual rep strengths and weaknesses.
- Before-the-CRM lead creation.

## What To Avoid

Avoid saying:

- "No one else is doing this."
- "This is the next Salesforce."
- "We will replace HubSpot immediately."
- "We own route planning."
- "We can patent the entire idea of AI route sales."

Those claims are too broad and easy to challenge.

## Better Claim

Use this:

> Prometheus is not trying to replace HubSpot. It is the lightweight field-intelligence layer for outside sales reps. It helps reps know where to go, what locations produce, how to save gas, and how to improve sales routes from real production data.

## Investor Reality

Competitors do not kill the idea. Competitors prove the market exists.

But Prometheus must prove:

- Reps use it daily.
- It increases sales results.
- It is simpler than broad enterprise tools.
- It can win a specific niche first.
- Fitness Connection or similar businesses would pay for it.

The question is not:

> Has anyone built field sales software?

The real question is:

> Can Prometheus produce a measurable LSM sales lift in a niche that existing platforms are not serving simply or specifically enough?
