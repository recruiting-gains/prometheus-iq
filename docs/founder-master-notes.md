# Prometheus Founder Master Notes

This file keeps the key business, brand, social, and protection notes for Prometheus in one place so they can be pulled up later.

## Brand Architecture

- Company: Prometheus
- First product: Prometheus IQ
- Public description: "Prometheus IQ, a Prometheus product."
- Prometheus is the parent brand that can support future products; Prometheus IQ is the field-sales intelligence application currently being built and tested.

## Core Story

Prometheus was born from real field sales, not from a boardroom.

The first version came from selling gym memberships outside the gym, doing local-store marketing, driving to businesses, apartments, and different areas trying to create sales. The problem was clear: most systems track the sale after it happens, but they do not really help the salesperson before the sale happens.

A CRM can store contacts, notes, and activity. Prometheus is being built to help answer the harder field-sales questions:

- Where should I go next?
- Which locations are worth revisiting?
- Which routes waste gas?
- Which places fit my strengths as a salesperson?
- Which outside activity is actually producing memberships?

Prometheus helps outside sales reps stop guessing. It tracks field activity, route quality, conversations, visits, and real sales results, then helps the rep understand what locations are actually producing. Over time, the goal is for Prometheus to learn from the salesperson and recommend better places to go based on strengths, weaknesses, and past results.

## Short Pitch

Prometheus IQ helps outside sales reps stop guessing where to go and start using real field data to sell smarter.

## Why The Name Prometheus

Prometheus represents giving fire to the people who need it. For outside sales reps, that fire is information, strategy, and intelligence. It is knowing where to go, what works, and how to stop wasting time and gas.

## What Separates Prometheus

Most competitors are broad field-sales tools. They help reps map routes, track visits, manage territories, and sync with CRMs.

Prometheus should be positioned as something sharper:

> Prometheus is not just "where did you go?" It is "where should you go next to produce more sales with less wasted gas?"

The strongest differences:

- Built for LSM and outside gym sales first.
- Focused on before-the-CRM intelligence.
- Learns the salesperson, not just the territory.
- Connects route planning to sales outcomes and gas usage.
- Designed to prove real work results through field testing.
- Cheaper and more focused than broad CRM platforms.
- Eventually customizable for other industries.

## Competitor Reality

Other companies already work in nearby spaces: SalesRabbit, SPOTIO, Badger Maps, Map My Customers, Outfield, Repsly, HubSpot, and Salesforce.

That does not kill Prometheus. It proves the market exists.

The key is not to claim nobody has ever done field-sales software. The stronger claim is:

> Big CRMs track sales after they happen, and field-sales apps help reps move around. Prometheus is built to learn which local places actually create gym memberships and route the rep toward higher-probability wins.

## Protection Strategy

Do not publicly reveal the algorithm, scoring formula, source code, full data model, or exact recommendation logic before speaking with a qualified attorney.

Prometheus may be protected through several layers:

- Trademark for the Prometheus brand.
- Copyright for code, designs, documentation, and pitch materials.
- Trade secrets for scoring formulas, prompts, logic, data models, and operating strategy.
- Contracts for beta testers, customers, contractors, and collaborators.
- Patent or provisional patent application if there is a truly new technical method.

Possible patent discussion areas:

- A unique scoring system for ranking locations.
- Rep-strength learning based on conversion history.
- Sales-outcome route optimization instead of only shortest-distance routing.
- Adaptive daily location generation.
- Gym-specific local-store-marketing intelligence.
- A feedback loop between field activity, sales outcome, and future route recommendations.

The broad idea of "an app that helps outside sales reps find better places and save gas" is probably too general. The more protectable angle is a specific technical method.

## Social Media Strategy

The strategy is:

> Sell the problem, show the journey, show the results, hide the formula.

Prometheus should not reveal:

- The algorithm.
- Exact scoring formula.
- Source code.
- Full app walkthroughs.
- Detailed screenshots of every feature.
- Private company data.
- Anything that makes the core concept easy to copy.

Prometheus can reveal:

- The field-sales problem.
- The founder journey.
- Real-world testing.
- General lessons from LSM.
- High-level results.
- Blurred dashboard clips.
- Route reflections without exact secret logic.

## Beta Plan

After roughly two weeks of refinement, give Prometheus to one coworker for a controlled test.

Before sharing:

- Make sure backup/export works.
- Create a simple onboarding note.
- Define what data the coworker must enter.
- Decide the test period.
- Keep their data separate from the founder's data.
- Use the results as a second proof point.

After three months:

- Compare performance before and after Prometheus.
- Compare route quality, gas/time savings, conversations, leads, and units.
- Build a simple case study.
- Prepare for Fitness Connection pitch.

## Investor Direction

A VC or strategic investor will care about:

- Real results.
- Repeatability.
- Evidence that more than one salesperson can use it.
- Market size.
- Competitive differentiation.
- Customer willingness to pay.
- Defensible data, workflows, or IP.
- Whether it can expand beyond one gym.

The strongest future story:

> Prometheus started with one outside salesperson, expanded to a small field test, showed measurable lift in LSM results, and became a focused field-sales intelligence product for businesses that need reps to create sales outside the CRM.
