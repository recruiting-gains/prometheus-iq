# Prometheus IQ Competitive Differentiation

Audience: Executives evaluating software purchases  
Company: Prometheus  
Product: Prometheus IQ  
Positioning: Field-sales intelligence for teams that need verified activity, better route decisions, and measurable outside-sales performance.

## 1. Why A Company Would Choose Prometheus Over Salesforce

Salesforce is a powerful enterprise CRM built to manage leads, accounts, opportunities, forecasts, dashboards, workflows, and customer records at scale.

Prometheus IQ is different. Prometheus focuses on the field-sales moment before a lead becomes part of the pipeline.

A company would choose Prometheus when the problem is:

- Reps do not know where to go next.
- Field activity is difficult to verify.
- Managers lack visibility into outside activity.
- LSM or outside-sales results are inconsistent.
- The company needs sharper decisions before, during, and after field visits.

Prometheus should not be positioned as a Salesforce replacement. It should be positioned as the field-sales intelligence layer that strengthens what happens before the CRM pipeline.

## 2. Why A Company Would Choose Prometheus Over HubSpot

HubSpot is a strong CRM and sales platform for managing contacts, deals, outreach, forecasting, reporting, and sales engagement.

Prometheus IQ is built for a narrower and more physical problem: helping field reps decide where to go, verify visits, log outcomes, and improve performance from real-world activity.

A company would choose Prometheus when it wants:

- Less guessing in field activity.
- GPS-verified business visits.
- Better accountability for outside sales work.
- Faster field logging without CRM overload.
- A simple way to measure LSM or field outreach performance.

HubSpot helps organize and manage sales activity. Prometheus helps make the outside activity more intentional before it becomes a CRM record.

## 3. Why A Company Would Choose Prometheus Over SPOTIO

SPOTIO is a mature field-sales execution platform with territory management, route optimization, activity tracking, pipeline visibility, CRM sync, and field-sales reporting.

Prometheus IQ should not pretend SPOTIO does not exist. SPOTIO is a serious competitor in field sales.

Prometheus can differentiate by being:

- Simpler for small controlled teams.
- More focused on lightweight pilot adoption.
- Built around verified visits, rep-level learning, and practical field decisions.
- Easier to customize around a specific business objective, such as gym LSM units.
- Positioned for teams that need a focused field intelligence pilot before buying a full enterprise field-sales platform.

The executive argument is not "SPOTIO cannot do field sales." The stronger argument is:

Prometheus gives teams a focused way to test field-sales intelligence quickly, verify activity, and prove whether outside-sales execution can improve before committing to a larger system.

## 4. Why A Company Would Choose Prometheus Over RouteIQ

If RouteIQ refers to a route-planning or route-optimization product, Prometheus should differentiate by not being route-only.

Route tools typically help answer:

- What is the best path?
- How can drive time be reduced?
- How can stops be sequenced?

Prometheus IQ is designed to answer a broader business question:

Which field activity is actually worth doing, and what happened when the rep went there?

Prometheus combines:

- Route Intelligence.
- GPS visit verification.
- Field outcome logging.
- Follow-up tracking.
- Manager visibility.
- Productivity measurement.

The difference is simple:

Route tools optimize movement. Prometheus aims to improve field-sales decisions and outcomes.

## 5. Prometheus' Strongest Differentiator

Prometheus' strongest differentiator is verified field-sales intelligence.

It connects the field day into one business story:

- Where the rep went.
- Whether the visit was verified.
- What happened.
- What result came from it.
- What should be reviewed next.

The value is not just mapping. The value is turning field activity into measurable business intelligence.

## 6. Prometheus' Contrarian Truth

Prometheus' contrarian truth:

In a world where companies are investing heavily in digital sales, AI, and CRM automation, face-to-face selling has become more scarce and therefore more valuable when it is focused, verified, and measured.

Most companies believe the future of sales is only more digital automation.

Prometheus believes the future also belongs to better-equipped field reps who can create trust, start real conversations, and generate opportunities in the physical world.

## 7. Prometheus' Defensible Advantage

Prometheus' defensible advantage is not that competitors cannot build maps, GPS, or dashboards.

The defensible advantage is the focused field-sales learning loop built from real rep activity, verified visits, business outcomes, and niche-specific execution.

Prometheus can defend itself by:

- Starting in a specific field-sales niche.
- Capturing real-world activity and outcomes.
- Building a dataset around field execution patterns.
- Staying simple enough for reps to actually use.
- Turning each pilot into sharper proof and better playbooks.

The advantage grows if Prometheus becomes the system that understands which field activities produce results for specific reps, locations, time windows, and business goals.

## 8. Executive Objections Prometheus Should Expect

### Objection 1: "Why not just use Salesforce or HubSpot?"

Executives may believe existing CRM tools already solve the problem.

### Objection 2: "Is this just a route-planning tool?"

Prometheus may be misunderstood as a map or routing app.

### Objection 3: "Will reps actually use it?"

Adoption is a serious concern. Field reps will reject tools that slow them down.

### Objection 4: "Does GPS create HR or privacy risk?"

GPS verification needs clear consent and boundaries.

### Objection 5: "Is the product secure enough?"

Executives will want to know access, data, and privacy controls are handled responsibly.

### Objection 6: "What proof do you have?"

Leadership will ask for measurable evidence before expansion.

### Objection 7: "Can a bigger competitor copy this?"

Salesforce, HubSpot, SPOTIO, or another platform could add similar surface-level features.

## 9. How Prometheus Should Answer Those Objections

### Answer 1: Salesforce and HubSpot are not the enemy.

Prometheus strengthens the activity that happens before the CRM pipeline. It helps field teams know where to go, verify activity, and capture outcomes before the opportunity becomes a traditional CRM record.

### Answer 2: Prometheus is not route-only.

Route planning is one piece. Prometheus focuses on decision-making, verified visits, outcomes, follow-ups, and field productivity.

### Answer 3: Adoption is a design requirement.

The pilot should prove whether reps can log activity quickly without feeling buried in admin work. If the workflow is not fast enough, the product must improve before expansion.

### Answer 4: GPS is visit verification, not surveillance.

GPS should only be captured when a rep intentionally logs a visit. Prometheus should not be positioned as continuous employee tracking.

### Answer 5: The pilot starts controlled.

Approved users only. Limited access. Private final report. No public use of company data without written approval.

### Answer 6: The pilot exists to create proof.

The 14-day pilot measures locations visited, GPS-verified visits, leads, units, follow-ups, conversion rate, time in field, and productivity per field session.

### Answer 7: Bigger companies can copy features, but not focused field proof.

Prometheus must win by moving faster in a narrow field-sales niche, gathering real-world outcomes, staying rep-friendly, and building specialized operating knowledge that broad CRMs do not prioritize.

## Executive Bottom Line

Prometheus IQ should not compete by claiming it is a better Salesforce, better HubSpot, or larger SPOTIO.

Prometheus should compete by owning a sharper wedge:

Verified field-sales intelligence for teams that need to know where reps should go, confirm what happened, and measure whether outside activity is improving.

The buying argument is:

If field sales matters to the business, then field activity should be guided, verified, measured, and improved.

Prometheus IQ is built for that gap.
