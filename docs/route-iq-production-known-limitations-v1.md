# Route IQ Production Known Limitations v1

Document ID: `route-iq-production-known-limitations-v1`
Owner: Prometheus Route IQ
Verified inputs: Prometheus Data, Intelligence Engine, Dashboard, Mobile, Infrastructure, CRM, and Overwatch owner evidence
Status: required disclosure for the bounded August 1 production activation

## Purpose

This document states the limits of the currently governed Route IQ production scope. It is not a beta disclaimer and must not be used to imply capabilities or evidence that do not exist.

## Current Product Boundary

Prometheus.IQ is the company. Prometheus Route IQ is the sole active production product. Route IQ structures field-operation context, representative actions, territories, customers, visits, and outcomes into governed evidence and decision-ready intelligence while preserving human control.

Route IQ does not replace a customer's CRM, route planner, mapping application, payroll system, workforce-surveillance system, or general-purpose AI assistant.

## Known Limitations

### Recommendation Evidence Is Insufficient

Current readiness is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Existing evidence does not prove causal uplift, recommendation superiority, or that a Route IQ choice will outperform a representative's choice.

Operational effect: no customer or user should be told that Route IQ has proven performance improvement or autonomous recommendation quality.

### Recommendation Influence Is Off

Recommendation influence is `OFF`. Route IQ does not use the governed shadow-readiness system to automatically change representative routes, actions, candidate visibility, or workflow.

Operational effect: a future influence activation requires separate evidence, verification, and Founder authorization.

### Prospective Collection Is Off

Prospective collection is `OFF` and active prospective cohorts are `0`.

Operational effect: no customer is enrolled in prospective shadow-evidence collection under the August 1 activation. Enabling collection or a cohort is outside launch scope.

### Founder-Only Intelligence Is Restricted

Founder CRM and Founder intelligence require exact immutable-principal authorization. Representatives and customer users are not authorized to access shadow decisions, readiness reports, governed learning outputs, or Founder CRM records.

Operational effect: role or access ambiguity fails closed. Anthony remains unmapped and has no access.

### Launch Capacity Is Deliberately Bounded

The August 1 production ceiling is two customer companies and 40 named customer users. A higher synthetic test capacity does not authorize a larger launch and is not an unlimited-scale claim.

Operational effect: a third company or forty-first named user requires a new Founder decision and new qualification of the expanded operating scope.

### Industry Performance Is Not Yet Proven

Configuration-driven workspace reuse has been verified, including a synthetic non-fitness configuration, but production performance across industries is not yet proven.

Operational effect: customer terminology and configuration may vary, but Prometheus must not claim proven outcomes for an industry without governed evidence.

### One Generic PWA

Route IQ uses one generic PWA named `Route IQ` rather than a separate application per customer. Customer identity, workspace, role, and terminology come from governed configuration and authenticated server context, not a customer-specific application build.

Operational effect: no customer-specific application, manifest, code fork, alternate hostname, or branded clone is included in this launch.

### Secure Connectivity Is Required

Protected PWA navigation requires a secure connection and current authentication. The service worker provides a bounded application shell but Route IQ is not represented as a full offline application.

Operational effect: network loss fails closed. Users must not repeatedly resubmit an ambiguous action after reconnecting.

### Device Location Is Evidence, Not Absolute Proof

Browser and operating-system location signals may be inaccurate or spoofed. Route IQ treats location context as evidence and does not claim continuous tracking or absolute proof of a visit.

Operational effect: managers must not use one location signal as the sole basis for an employment, disciplinary, or customer decision.

### Evidence Quality Controls Intelligence Quality

Intelligence depends on governed evidence completeness, freshness, chronology, identity, isolation, and integrity. Missing or conflicting evidence must reduce confidence or fail closed.

Operational effect: insufficient evidence must not be presented as a confident recommendation or prediction.

### Retention Is Bounded

Raw Route IQ field-session and prospective evidence may be retained for no more than 18 months from creation. An authenticated customer contract may shorten the period but may not expand it without separate Founder authorization. Legal hold may pause disposition.

Operational effect: customer onboarding must disclose the applicable period. Deletion, exclusion, correction, and administrative disposition remain auditable and Data-owned; records are not silently rewritten.

### Founder Research Is Not Customer Validation

Fitness Connection evidence is classified as `FOUNDER_FIELD_RESEARCH`. Fitness Connection is not represented as a Route IQ customer, pilot, partner, sponsor, or endorser.

Operational effect: that evidence cannot be used to claim a customer relationship, commercial validation, endorsement, or performance result.

### Support Is Bounded By Approved Coverage

The August 1 activation requires a named support owner, support channel, coverage window, and escalation contact. It does not create an unapproved 24-hour support SLA.

Operational effect: customer-facing response commitments require explicit Founder and customer agreement.

### Production Changes Remain Founder-Gated

Deployment, rollback, access changes, production-data correction, notification retry, retention expansion, scope expansion, collection, cohorts, recommendation influence, and Anthony mapping are not implied by normal support or onboarding.

Operational effect: operators may stop new activation and gather read-only evidence, but production mutations require the exact governed authorization.

## Required Customer Acknowledgments

Before activation, the customer decision-maker acknowledges:

- Route IQ's human-control and non-superiority boundary;
- collection `OFF`, cohorts `0`, and recommendation influence `OFF`;
- the two-company and 40-user launch ceiling;
- one generic PWA and secure-connectivity requirement;
- permitted data and location-evidence limitations;
- applicable retention and legal-hold handling;
- support channel, hours, and escalation process;
- that feature requests and support reports do not authorize product or production changes.

## Required Founder Decisions

Only the Founder may approve:

- the exact customer companies and named-user roster;
- final production go/no-go;
- any deployment or rollback;
- any Access, role, setting, binding, migration, route, or traffic mutation;
- any production-data correction, disposition, deletion, replay, or export;
- any scope, retention, collection, cohort, recommendation, Founder-intelligence, or Anthony change.

Customer approval is also required for that customer's roster, territory, permitted data, device policy, retention shortening, training, support contacts, and activation date. Neither party may unilaterally expand the governed product boundary.

## Disclosure Rule

Use plain language. Do not describe Route IQ as a beta, but do not hide a limitation behind production terminology. Do not claim a capability, relationship, result, scale, security property, or intelligence quality that current owner evidence does not prove.
