## 2. Executive Summary

Prometheus IQ is a field-sales intelligence platform designed to help outside sales teams know where to go, verify activity, learn from results, and improve performance over time.

Most sales systems are built around tracking leads after they already exist. Prometheus IQ focuses on the work that happens before the pipeline: field activity, location decisions, visit outcomes, and follow-up opportunities.

For Fitness Connection, the opportunity is to test whether a focused field-sales tool can improve LSM productivity, reduce wasted field time, increase visibility, and help sales teams generate more measurable results from outside marketing.

The proposed next step is a controlled private pilot with approved users, limited access, clear success metrics, and no disruption to current sales systems.

## 3. Why Now

Fitness Connection already invests time and energy into LSM activity. The opportunity is to make that activity easier to measure, verify, and improve.

The sales environment is also changing. Digital tools are everywhere, but face-to-face field activity remains valuable when it is focused, consistent, and measurable.

Prometheus IQ gives leadership a way to test whether outside activity can become more structured without making the rep's job harder.

## 4. The Business Problem

Field sales and LSM activity are valuable, but they are often difficult to manage consistently.

Common challenges include:

- Reps spend time deciding where to go instead of taking action.
- Field visits are not always easy to verify.
- Managers may not have clear visibility into what happened in the field.
- Follow-up opportunities can be lost after a conversation.
- Good locations, time windows, and visit patterns are not always captured in a usable way.
- Current CRM tools are helpful for pipeline management, but they are not always built around the real-time needs of reps in the field.

The result is that valuable field activity can become hard to measure, repeat, and improve.

## 5. The Prometheus IQ Solution

Prometheus IQ gives field sales teams a simple operating layer for outside sales activity.

At a high level, the platform helps a rep:

- See a focused field plan.
- Visit real locations.
- Log conversations, leads, visits, and results.
- Attach GPS-verified visit context when appropriate.
- Track follow-up opportunities.
- Give managers a clearer view of field activity.
- Improve future route decisions based on verified outcomes.

Prometheus IQ is not intended to replace existing CRM systems. It is designed to strengthen the activity that happens before, during, and after field outreach.

In simple terms:

Prometheus IQ helps field teams turn outside activity into measurable intelligence.

## 6. GPS Verification and Route Intelligence

### GPS Verification

Prometheus IQ supports GPS pin-drop verification for business visits.

This is designed to confirm that a rep was physically near a logged visit location at the time of entry. It is not designed as continuous employee tracking.

Recommended pilot standard:

- Location is captured only when the rep intentionally logs a visit.
- The rep understands what is being captured.
- GPS data is used for visit verification and activity review.
- Managers only review information relevant to authorized business activity.

The purpose is accountability and clarity, not surveillance.

### Route Intelligence

Prometheus IQ provides field direction by helping reps focus on promising locations and practical next steps.

The goal is to reduce wasted movement and improve field productivity. The system helps answer:

- Where should the rep go next?
- Which locations deserve attention?
- What outcomes are being produced from field activity?
- What follow-up opportunities should not be missed?

The pilot should evaluate outcomes, not internal technology. The focus is whether the tool helps reps work smarter in the field.

## 7. Proposed Pilot Structure

### Pilot Objective

Test whether Prometheus IQ can improve LSM consistency, visibility, and productivity in a controlled field-sales environment.

### Recommended Pilot Setup

Start small:

- 3 approved users.
- 1 approved club or market area.
- 14 days of testing.
- 1 weekly review during the pilot.
- 1 final results report after completion.

### User Types

- Sales rep / consultant: logs visits, conversations, leads, outcomes, and follow-ups.
- Manager / reviewer: reviews activity summaries, route performance, and measurable outcomes.

### Pilot Rules

- Use approved testers only.
- Use approved locations and business categories.
- No sensitive company data should be uploaded unless approved.
- GPS verification should only happen during intentional visit logging.
- Results should be reviewed weekly.
- The pilot should be measured against clear success metrics.

### Weekly Review

The weekly review should focus on:

- Whether approved users are using the tool consistently.
- Whether visit logging feels fast enough for the field.
- Whether GPS verification is useful and appropriate.
- Whether managers gain better visibility into LSM activity.
- Whether early results show stronger field productivity.

### Pilot Deliverables

At the end of the pilot, Prometheus will provide a short summary showing:

- Number of visits logged.
- Conversations recorded.
- Leads generated.
- Units generated.
- Follow-ups created.
- Best performing location categories.
- Time windows with strongest results.
- Rep feedback on ease of use.
- Manager feedback on visibility and usefulness.

## 8. Risk Controls

The pilot should remain controlled, limited, and transparent.

Recommended controls:

- Approved users only.
- Approved pilot duration only.
- GPS captured only when a user intentionally logs a visit.
- No continuous location tracking.
- User consent before participation.
- Manager visibility limited to authorized pilot activity.
- No public use of Fitness Connection data, results, testimonials, or case studies without written approval.
- No sensitive company data entered unless approved by leadership.
- Final report shared only with approved stakeholders.

These controls are designed to make the pilot useful while protecting Fitness Connection, participating employees, and Prometheus.

## 9. Data Ownership and Confidentiality

Recommended pilot data understanding:

- Fitness Connection owns its business data.
- Prometheus owns the Prometheus IQ software, product, brand, and intellectual property.
- Pilot data should be used only to evaluate the pilot unless both sides approve broader use.
- Any public case study, testimonial, statistic, or reference to Fitness Connection should require separate written permission.
- Prometheus should not disclose private Fitness Connection information to other businesses.
- Fitness Connection should not share, copy, or reverse engineer Prometheus IQ outside the approved pilot review process.

This section is intended to create a clear separation between company business data and Prometheus product ownership.

## 11. Pilot Success Metrics

The pilot should be judged on simple, measurable outcomes.

Recommended success metrics:

1. Activity Completion  
   Did approved users consistently log field visits?

2. GPS-Verified Visits  
   What percentage of visits included intentional GPS verification?

3. Conversations Logged  
   How many real business or prospect conversations were recorded?

4. Leads Generated  
   How many potential opportunities came from field activity?

5. Units Generated  
   How many memberships or measurable sales outcomes came from LSM activity?

6. Follow-Up Capture  
   Were follow-up opportunities recorded instead of lost?

7. Rep Adoption  
   Did the tool feel fast enough for real field use?

8. Manager Visibility  
   Did leadership gain a clearer picture of field activity and outcomes?

9. Route Usefulness  
   Did Route Intelligence help reps make better field decisions?

10. Productivity Signal  
   Did the pilot show stronger output per visit, conversation, or field session?

### Success Threshold

The pilot should be considered successful if it shows clear evidence in at least three areas:

- Approved users consistently log field activity.
- GPS-verified visits improve accountability and clarity.
- Managers gain better visibility into LSM execution.
- The tool feels fast enough for real field use.
- Field activity produces measurable leads, units, follow-ups, or productivity improvement.

The pilot does not need to prove company-wide rollout immediately. It only needs to prove whether Prometheus IQ is worth a larger controlled evaluation.
