# Prometheus IQ Pilot Approval Package

Purpose: Initial management, HR, legal, and operations review  
Company: Prometheus  
Product: Prometheus IQ  
Use case: Controlled private pilot for field sales / LSM activity

Important note: This package is an initial business review draft. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before signature or official use.

## 1. Pilot Agreement

### Pilot Purpose

Prometheus IQ will be tested in a limited private pilot to evaluate whether it can improve field-sales visibility, GPS-verified activity, route usefulness, visit logging, follow-up capture, and measurable LSM outcomes.

### Pilot Scope

- Pilot duration: 14 days.
- Approved users: 3 users.
- Review cadence: 1 weekly review during the pilot.
- Final deliverable: private pilot results report.
- Access: limited to approved users and approved reviewers.
- Use case: field sales / LSM activity only.

### Pilot Responsibilities

Prometheus will:

- Provide access to Prometheus IQ for approved users.
- Provide simple user instructions.
- Support basic pilot questions during the pilot period.
- Prepare a final pilot summary for approved stakeholders.
- Avoid public use of pilot results unless written permission is granted.

Fitness Connection / pilot organization will:

- Identify approved users.
- Confirm management approval before employee participation.
- Confirm whether HR/legal review is required before launch.
- Ensure users understand pilot expectations.
- Review pilot results privately.

### Pilot Limits

- This is not a company-wide rollout.
- This is not a replacement for existing CRM or company systems.
- This is not a guarantee of sales results.
- This does not authorize public use of company data, employee information, results, testimonials, or brand references.
- Either side may stop the pilot if operational, legal, HR, privacy, or security concerns arise.

### Pilot Success Review

At the end of the pilot, the parties may review:

- Whether users consistently logged field activity.
- Whether GPS-verified visits were useful and appropriate.
- Whether managers gained clearer visibility.
- Whether reps found the workflow fast enough.
- Whether field activity produced measurable leads, units, follow-ups, or productivity improvement.

## 3. Security Summary

Prometheus IQ is being prepared for controlled private pilot use with a focus on limited access, careful data handling, and simple user controls.

### Current Security Position

- Cloudflare protected deployment has been verified.
- Access is intended for approved users only.
- GPS verification is intentional visit-based verification, not continuous tracking.
- Pilot information should be limited to approved business activity.
- Sensitive company data should not be entered unless leadership approves.

### Security Principles

Prometheus IQ should follow these principles during pilot use:

- Minimum necessary data collection.
- Approved users only.
- Approved reviewers only.
- No continuous employee tracking.
- No public sharing of pilot data without written approval.
- Private final reporting to approved stakeholders only.
- Prompt review of any security, privacy, or access concern.

### Operational Security Controls

Recommended pilot controls:

- Confirm approved user list before launch.
- Confirm approved reviewer list before launch.
- Confirm who may receive the final report.
- Require user consent before participation.
- Keep pilot data private.
- Stop the pilot if a material security or privacy concern appears.

## 5. Pilot Exit Plan

The pilot should have a clear ending process so both sides understand what happens after the test.

### Normal End Of Pilot

At the end of 14 days:

1. Prometheus prepares a private final report.
2. Approved leadership reviews results.
3. Users provide feedback on ease of use.
4. Management determines whether to stop, extend, or expand the pilot.

### Early Stop Conditions

Either side may request that the pilot stop early if:

- HR or legal concerns arise.
- A user is uncomfortable continuing.
- Data security concerns appear.
- The tool is not being used as approved.
- The pilot becomes operationally disruptive.
- Leadership decides the pilot is no longer appropriate.

### Data Handling At Exit

At pilot exit, the parties should confirm:

- Who receives the final report.
- Whether any data should be deleted, retained, or exported.
- Whether any user access should be removed.
- Whether any follow-up review is needed.
- Whether any public reference is permitted. Default answer: no public reference unless separately approved in writing.

### Post-Pilot Decision Options

After the final review, leadership may choose one of four paths:

1. Stop the pilot with no further action.
2. Extend the pilot with the same users.
3. Expand to a larger controlled pilot.
4. Begin discussion of a formal business relationship.
