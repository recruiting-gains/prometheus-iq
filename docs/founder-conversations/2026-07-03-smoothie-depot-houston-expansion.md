# Founder Conversation - Smoothie Depot Houston Expansion

Date: 2026-07-03

Purpose: preserve the founder discussion about Smoothie Depot's Houston-area expansion opportunity versus Smoothie King.

## Conversation Summary

The founder asked for an analyst-style view of how many Smoothie Depot locations exist in Houston compared with Smoothie King, what Smoothie King's recent revenue base looks like, and what Smoothie Depot would need to do to expand across the Houston area.

## Verified Location Baseline

Smoothie Depot:

- Houston city locations identified from Smoothie Depot's own location page: 2.
- Houston-area / nearby brick-and-mortar locations identified from Smoothie Depot's own location page: 8, plus mobile service.
- Listed locations include Fuqua, Pasadena, Webster, Bissonnet, Katy, League City / 646 League City, Tuscan Lakes / League City, La Porte, and mobile service.

Smoothie King:

- Official Smoothie King Houston locator listed 37 Houston locations.

## Revenue Estimate

Smoothie King is private/franchised, so Houston store-level revenue is not publicly disclosed.

Best estimate used:

```text
Houston Smoothie King revenue estimate = official Houston store count x Smoothie King disclosed average annual net sales per franchised unit
```

Projection assumption: 37 Houston stores held flat, with 2.5% annual AUV growth. Each additional mature Houston Smoothie King store would add roughly $0.68M to $0.71M in annual sales under this model.

## Strategic Readout

Smoothie King has overwhelming density in Houston. Smoothie Depot is currently a local/regional challenger, not yet a density-equivalent competitor.

Approximate competitive tiers for Smoothie Depot:

| Goal | Store Count | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Local challenger | 15-20 | Noticeable regional brand |
| Serious competitor | 25-35 | Real Houston coverage |
| Houston-area leader | 40-50+ | Strong enough to compete with Smoothie King density |
| Metro dominance | 60+ | Covers Houston, suburbs, campuses, gyms, hospitals, and retail corridors |

Founder takeaway:

Smoothie Depot probably needs 35-50 Houston-area stores to seriously challenge Smoothie King and 60+ to credibly dominate the full metro.

## Recommended Expansion Priorities

Smoothie Depot should not expand randomly. It should expand by clusters and territory coverage.

Priority expansion zones:

- Inner Loop / premium visibility: Heights, Montrose, Midtown, Rice Village, Medical Center, Downtown.
- West Houston money corridor: Memorial, Energy Corridor, CityCentre, Katy, Cypress.
- Southwest growth: Sugar Land, Missouri City, Bellaire, Stafford.
- North Houston: Spring, The Woodlands, Tomball, Humble / Atascocita.
- Campus / fitness / healthcare anchors: UH, Rice, Texas Southern, Medical Center, gym corridors.

Operational needs before aggressive expansion:

- Clean franchise package with proven unit economics.
- Standardized store design, menu, training, suppliers, and POS.
- Territory map to prevent franchisee cannibalization.
- Strong digital ordering, loyalty, and app presence.
- Local influencer and fitness partnerships.
- Gym, school, and hospital catering.
- A hero product Smoothie King does not own.
- Faster service times and lower price perception.
- Better Google Maps and SEO presence per location.
- Real estate strategy around gyms, schools, hospitals, and commuter corridors.

## Positioning Recommendation

Smoothie Depot should not copy Smoothie King. It should position as:

> Houston-born smoothie brand. Local, fast, customizable, protein-forward, and community-driven.

Smoothie King is the national incumbent. Smoothie Depot's best path is to become the Houston hometown challenger.
