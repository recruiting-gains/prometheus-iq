# Prometheus Investor Readiness Plan

## Goal

Build Prometheus into a credible investor and enterprise-sales story by combining real field results, multi-rep usage, social proof, intellectual property work, and a clear first-customer strategy.

## Core Investor Story

Prometheus is not just an app idea. It is a field-sales intelligence system built from real outside-sales activity.

By the time Prometheus is shown to a VC or Fitness Connection leadership, the strongest story should be:

> Prometheus was tested by three outside sales reps at one gym. Over three months, it helped improve LSM sales by using real field data to plan better routes, reduce wasted stops, save gas, identify better locations, and show which outside activity produced units.

## Proof Package

The pitch should include:

- Data from Cruz.
- Data from two other gym coworkers.
- Names or roles of Fitness Connection decision-makers to approach: Phil, President, and Larry, COO.
- Three months of logged field activity.
- Before/after comparison against the normal HubSpot-only workflow.
- Units produced through Prometheus-planned routes.
- Best location categories.
- Best time windows.
- Locations removed because they wasted time.
- Locations revisited because they worked.
- Estimated time/gas saved.
- Screenshots of the app.
- Weekly result summaries.
- Coworker feedback.
- Instagram/social media feedback.
- Feedback or interest from leaders in other industries.
- Patent/provisional patent documentation if available.
- Proposed Fitness Connection pilot or subscription structure.

## Three-Rep Data Is Important

Investor concern:

> Did Prometheus work because Cruz is unusually motivated, or because the product makes reps better?

Using three people helps answer that.

Track each rep separately:

- Rep name or code.
- Days used.
- Places visited.
- Conversations.
- Leads.
- Same-day units.
- Later units.
- Units per visit.
- Best category.
- Best time window.
- Route changes made because of Prometheus.

Then show combined results:

- Total reps: 3
- Total days used
- Total visits logged
- Total conversations
- Total leads
- Total units
- Sales increase compared with prior LSM process

## Instagram And Market Interest

The Instagram page should build interest without launching too early.

Use it to show:

- The field-sales problem.
- Why outside reps waste time and gas.
- How route planning can improve sales.
- Anonymous lessons from real field tests.
- Screenshots or mockups without exposing sensitive company data.
- Waitlist interest.
- Comments, DMs, and feedback from salespeople.

Do not overpromise before the product is ready.

Good content angles:

- "Outside sales reps waste too much time guessing where to go."
- "The best sales route is not always the closest route. It is the route with the best production history."
- "Most CRMs track leads after they exist. Prometheus helps reps find where to create them."
- "Field sales needs route intelligence, not just contact management."

Investor value:

- Shows people understand the pain.
- Shows early demand.
- Shows the brand can attract outside-sales reps.
- Creates evidence of market pull before launch.

## Other Industry Interest

If leaders in other industries have shown interest in Prometheus, document it.

Track:

- Person or company type.
- Industry.
- What pain they confirmed.
- Whether they asked for a demo.
- Whether they asked about pricing.
- Whether they would test it later.

Use this as early market validation, not guaranteed sales.

Strong investor framing:

> Prometheus is starting with Fitness Connection because that is where the first real data is being created, but early conversations with leaders in other industries show the outside-sales route problem is not gym-specific.

## Patent And IP Positioning

If patent work is completed or in progress, present it carefully.

Do not say:

> We are protected, so nobody can copy us.

Say:

> We have started formal IP protection around our field-sales route intelligence workflow, and we are building proprietary data from real outside-sales activity.

The strongest protection is not only a patent. It is:

- Real user data.
- Workflow knowledge.
- Fast product iteration.
- Distribution.
- Customer relationships.
- Case studies.
- Brand trust.

Important: work with a qualified patent attorney before making legal claims in a pitch.

## No Other Gym Contract

The "no other gym" idea can be powerful, but it must be handled carefully.

Possible framing for Fitness Connection:

> If Fitness Connection becomes the first enterprise partner, Prometheus can offer category exclusivity for gyms/fitness clubs for a defined period, in exchange for a paid pilot, rollout support, or a larger subscription commitment.

Do not give away permanent exclusivity too cheaply.

Better structure:

- Limited exclusivity by industry.
- Limited exclusivity by geography.
- Limited exclusivity by time.
- Exclusivity tied to payment and rollout commitment.

Example:

- 12-month gym-industry exclusivity in selected markets.
- Fitness Connection must run a paid pilot or paid subscription.
- Exclusivity renews only if usage and payment milestones are met.

Reason:

Prometheus may eventually be useful to many gyms. A broad permanent "no other gym" contract could reduce company value unless Fitness Connection pays enough to justify it.

## Larry As Potential Strategic Investor

Larry could be valuable as a strategic investor because he understands Fitness Connection operations and outside membership sales from a leadership position.

However, if Larry is COO and also a potential investor, separate the conversations.

Recommended approach:

- First pitch Prometheus to Phil and Larry as a business tool for Fitness Connection.
- If Larry personally expresses interest in investing, schedule a separate conversation.
- Keep any investment discussion transparent and separate from a Fitness Connection purchasing decision.
- Get legal guidance before accepting investment from someone who may influence the company buying Prometheus.

Investor framing:

> Larry is a potential strategic investor because he understands the market and the operational pain. Any investment discussion would be handled separately and transparently from Fitness Connection's decision to pilot or purchase Prometheus.

## VC Reaction If These Pieces Are Real

If Prometheus has:

- Three months of data.
- Three reps using it.
- A meaningful sales lift.
- Coworker adoption.
- Instagram interest.
- Interest from leaders in other industries.
- Patent/IP work started.
- A possible enterprise customer conversation.

Then a VC would likely take the meeting seriously.

They may still not invest immediately, but Prometheus would move from "idea" to "early evidence."

## Biggest Investor Questions

Be ready to answer:

- Did sales increase because of Prometheus or because reps worked harder?
- Can this work at another gym location?
- Can an average rep use it without Cruz?
- Who pays: rep, club manager, region, or corporate?
- How much would Fitness Connection pay monthly?
- How hard is it to switch from a spreadsheet or CRM report?
- What data does Prometheus own?
- What is the product moat?
- Why would HubSpot not build this?
- Can it expand beyond gyms?

## Best Next Step

Before talking to a VC, get a small but strong proof package:

- Three reps.
- Three months.
- Clean before/after data.
- Screenshots.
- Coworker testimonials.
- Instagram interest.
- Clear Fitness Connection pilot proposal.
- Simple pricing idea.
- IP status reviewed by an attorney.

Then the pitch becomes:

> We built Prometheus from real field-sales pain. Three reps tested it for three months. It improved LSM sales compared with the normal HubSpot workflow. We have early social interest, IP work underway, and a path to selling it first to Fitness Connection before expanding to other outside-sales industries.
