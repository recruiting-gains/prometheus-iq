# Prometheus IQ Pilot Success Metrics Framework

Pilot: 14-day Fitness Connection private pilot  
Users: 3 approved users  
Use case: LSM field sales activity  
Product: Prometheus IQ

## 1. Executive Summary

The purpose of the Prometheus IQ pilot is to determine whether a focused field-sales operating system can improve LSM execution, visibility, accountability, and measurable sales outcomes.

The pilot should answer one executive question:

Did Prometheus IQ help the team make better use of field time and produce clearer, more measurable LSM results?

The pilot is not judged only by units sold. It should also measure whether the team visited more intentional locations, verified activity more clearly, generated more leads, completed follow-ups, and improved productivity per field session.

## 3. Success Thresholds

The pilot should be judged across activity, adoption, visibility, and results.

### Minimum Success

Prometheus IQ shows enough value to justify further controlled testing.

Minimum success signals:

- All 3 approved users participate.
- At least 70% of field visits are logged.
- At least 60% of logged visits include GPS verification when appropriate.
- Users report that visit logging is understandable and usable in the field.
- Leadership receives clearer visibility than before the pilot.
- At least one measurable business outcome is captured: leads, units, follow-ups, or improved productivity per session.

### Strong Success

Prometheus IQ shows clear operational value and should be considered for a larger pilot.

Strong success signals:

- All 3 approved users participate consistently.
- At least 85% of field visits are logged.
- At least 75% of logged visits include GPS verification when appropriate.
- The pilot produces measurable leads and membership units.
- Follow-up opportunities are captured and completed.
- Managers can identify which locations, categories, or time windows performed best.
- Reps report that the tool helps reduce guessing about where to go.

### Exceptional Success

Prometheus IQ shows strong enough value to justify executive discussion about expansion.

Exceptional success signals:

- All 3 users consistently log activity across the full 14 days.
- At least 90% of field visits are logged.
- At least 85% of logged visits include GPS verification when appropriate.
- The pilot produces a clear lift in leads, units, follow-ups, or productivity per field session compared with baseline activity.
- Leadership can identify repeatable location or time-window patterns.
- Reps and managers both report that Prometheus IQ improves field execution.
- The final report creates a clear business case for a larger controlled pilot.
