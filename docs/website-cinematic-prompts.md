# Prometheus Website Cinematic Direction

## Depth

Build a full-screen outdoor field-sales scene with four readable depth planes: distant business district, street-level route network, verified business nodes, and a mobile command interface in the foreground. Preserve dark negative space for copy. Use forest green, ember orange, warm gold, and restrained teal light. The scene must communicate real movement through a physical market without exposing private data or product logic.

## Parallax

As the visitor scrolls, move the distant city slowly, the route network at medium speed, and the phone interface fastest. Keep all layers anchored to the same world direction so the effect feels like camera depth, not independent floating objects. Use small pointer movement only on precise devices, and disable nonessential motion when reduced motion is requested.

## Camera

Begin with a wide establishing shot behind the founder message. Push forward along the illuminated route as outcomes enter, bank gently toward verified nodes during the product section, then settle into a stable command view for the company and contact sections. Use smooth scrubbed motion, restrained rotation, warm directional light, teal node light, soft shadows, and no hard cuts.
