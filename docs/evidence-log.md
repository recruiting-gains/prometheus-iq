It does not, by itself, establish how many LSM outings occurred, whether all listed units were generated through Prometheus IQ-directed routes, or the causal lift from using Prometheus IQ. HubSpot contacts and LSM units also represent different stages and channels, so their raw totals should not be presented as a direct performance comparison. To make a defensible product claim, match dated Prometheus IQ visit records to these units and compare them with a documented pre-product baseline or control period.
