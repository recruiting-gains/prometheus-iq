# Prometheus Pricing Strategy Playbook

Scenario: Pilot succeeds  
Customer: Fitness Connection  
Initial scope: Houston market  
Initial users: 70 seats  
Agreement target: 6 months  
Owner: Prometheus LLC  
Purpose: Set pricing strategy that supports adoption, retention, expansion, and long-term company growth.

## 1. Founding Partner Pricing Strategy

Fitness Connection should be treated as a potential founding partner, not just a first customer.

The goal is not to extract the highest possible price immediately. The goal is to:

- Convert a successful pilot into a real paid agreement.
- Keep adoption friction low.
- Build a strong case study if approved.
- Prove Prometheus IQ can operate across a larger market.
- Create a path to expansion after measurable results.

Recommended approach:

- Offer a 6-month founding partner agreement.
- Price below mature enterprise software.
- Make the offer time-limited and tied to early partnership status.
- Include a clear renewal step after the first 6 months.
- Avoid lifetime discounts that limit future revenue.

Positioning language:

"Because this would be the first larger market deployment, I would propose founding partner pricing for the first 6 months. The goal is to prove adoption, impact, and scalability before moving into standard long-term pricing."

## 2. Installation / Setup Fee Strategy

The setup fee should cover onboarding, configuration, training, reporting setup, and support time.

Do not make the setup fee so high that it blocks approval. Do not make it zero if meaningful work is required.

Recommended founding partner setup fee:

- Low range: $1,500
- Recommended: $2,500
- High range: $5,000

For Fitness Connection Houston with 70 users, recommended setup fee:

**$2,500 one-time setup fee**

What it covers:

- Account / access preparation.
- User onboarding materials.
- Manager onboarding.
- Pilot-to-paid transition support.
- Initial reporting structure.
- First-month support.

Optional move:

If leadership pushes back, reduce or waive part of the setup fee in exchange for a stronger 6-month commitment.

## 3. Monthly Per-Seat Pricing Strategy

Per-seat pricing should be simple and easy to approve.

Pricing should reflect:

- Value created through better field activity.
- GPS-verified accountability.
- Route Intelligence.
- Manager visibility.
- Productivity measurement.
- Early-stage product maturity.

Recommended founding partner range:

- Low: $39/user/month
- Recommended: $49/user/month
- High: $79/user/month

For 70 users:

- $39/user/month = $2,730/month
- $49/user/month = $3,430/month
- $79/user/month = $5,530/month

Recommended first 6-month offer:

**$49/user/month for 70 users**

Estimated monthly recurring revenue:

**$3,430/month**

Estimated 6-month recurring revenue:

**$20,580**

With setup fee:

**$23,080 total over 6 months**

This is meaningful early revenue without pricing so high that adoption becomes the enemy.

## 4. Renewal Pricing Strategy

The 6-month agreement should have a clear renewal path.

Do not promise the same founding partner price forever.

Recommended renewal structure:

- Founding partner price applies for first 6 months.
- Renewal price increases only if value is proven.
- Renewal discussion begins 45 days before agreement end.
- Renewal may include expanded reporting, additional clubs, or more users.

Suggested renewal pricing:

- Renew at $59/user/month if adoption and results are strong.
- Renew at $69/user/month if the deployment expands beyond the initial market.
- Keep $49/user/month only if needed to secure a longer 12-month commitment.

Renewal language:

"The first 6 months would be founding partner pricing. If the results justify continuing, we can review a renewal based on adoption, usage, and measurable field-sales outcomes."

## 5. Expansion Pricing Strategy

Expansion should be based on proof.

Do not push expansion before the Houston market is stable.

Recommended expansion path:

1. Houston market: 70 users.
2. Additional Texas markets.
3. Regional rollout.
4. Company-wide rollout.

Expansion pricing options:

- Same per-seat price for the first added market if expansion happens within the first 6 months.
- Slightly higher per-seat price for new markets after the first 6 months.
- Volume discounts only after a minimum user threshold.

Suggested expansion structure:

- 1-100 users: $49-$69/user/month
- 101-250 users: $45-$59/user/month
- 251+ users: enterprise agreement

Do not over-discount too early. Expansion should reward commitment, not weaken the business model.

## 6. When To Increase Pricing

Increase pricing when one or more of these are true:

- Prometheus IQ has proven measurable results.
- Managers rely on the reports.
- Reps use the system consistently.
- The product supports multiple teams or markets.
- Cloud accounts and secure multi-user management are mature.
- Support and onboarding requirements increase.
- Prometheus has more than one paying customer.
- Case studies or approved proof points exist.

Do not increase pricing only because you are excited.

Increase pricing when the product is:

- More proven.
- More trusted.
- More adopted.
- More operationally expensive to support.
- More valuable to the customer.

## 7. How To Discuss Pricing With Executives

Pricing should be discussed after value is clear.

Do not lead with price. Lead with the business case:

- More measurable LSM execution.
- Better field accountability.
- Better visibility for managers.
- Less wasted field time.
- Stronger field-sales decision-making.

Executive pricing script:

"If the pilot proves that Prometheus IQ improves field visibility, accountability, and measurable LSM outcomes, I would propose a 6-month founding partner agreement for the Houston market. Because Fitness Connection would be an early partner, the pricing would be intentionally adoption-friendly: a one-time setup fee and a simple per-user monthly rate. The goal would be to prove value, support adoption, and review expansion only after results are clear."

If asked for numbers:

"For 70 Houston users, I would recommend founding partner pricing around $49 per user per month, plus a $2,500 setup fee. That keeps the initial 6-month agreement meaningful but reasonable while we prove long-term value."

## 8. Common Pricing Mistakes Founders Make

Avoid these mistakes:

- Pricing too high before trust is established.
- Pricing too low forever.
- Giving away the product without a clear conversion path.
- Offering lifetime discounts.
- Charging for every small feature instead of charging for outcomes.
- Talking price before proving value.
- Overcomplicating pricing tiers.
- Failing to charge for setup, onboarding, and support.
- Expanding before the first deployment is stable.
- Discounting without receiving something in return.

Good discount tradeoffs:

- Longer contract.
- Case study permission.
- Executive feedback.
- Approved testimonial.
- Faster rollout decision.
- Upfront payment.

## 9. Recommended Pricing Tiers

### Individual User

Use case:

- Solo rep, founder, independent field seller, or very small test.

Recommended price:

- $29-$49/user/month

Positioning:

- Simple field logging and route guidance for one user.

### Small Team

Use case:

- 3-25 users.
- Local business.
- Small field-sales team.
- Controlled pilot or early team deployment.

Recommended price:

- $49-$79/user/month

Setup fee:

- $500-$2,500 depending on onboarding needs.

Positioning:

- Field visibility, GPS-verified activity, Route Intelligence, follow-up tracking, and manager review.

### Enterprise

Use case:

- 50+ users.
- Multiple managers.
- Multiple locations or markets.
- Reporting and support expectations.

Recommended price:

- $49-$99/user/month depending on maturity, support, reporting, and scale.

Setup fee:

- $2,500-$15,000 depending on scope.

Positioning:

- Field-sales intelligence layer for outside-sales teams that need accountability, decision-making, and measurable performance improvement.

## 10. Pricing Philosophy

Prometheus should price for long-term trust, not short-term extraction.

The pricing philosophy:

- Make the first paid agreement easy to approve.
- Charge enough to prove this is a real business.
- Keep the first expansion path simple.
- Increase pricing as proof, adoption, and enterprise value grow.
- Protect long-term upside by avoiding permanent early discounts.
- Tie pricing to measurable business outcomes, not feature count.

Prometheus should become more valuable over time as it proves:

- Reps adopt it.
- Managers use it.
- Field activity becomes more measurable.
- Results improve.
- Customers expand usage.

Recommended founding partner offer for Fitness Connection Houston:

- 70 users.
- 6-month term.
- $49/user/month.
- $2,500 setup fee.
- Total 6-month value: $23,080.

Recommended internal stance:

If Fitness Connection becomes a serious founding partner, optimize for the paid relationship, proof, and expansion path. Do not overprice the first agreement. Do not give it away without structure.
