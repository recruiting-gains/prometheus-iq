# Prometheus Founder Operating System

Purpose: Help the founder grow Prometheus while protecting energy, marriage, health, AI school progress, and customer trust.

Core principle: Prometheus grows faster when the founder is consistent, clear, rested, and focused. Burnout is not a badge of honor. Burnout slows the company down.

## Founder Priorities

Prometheus must be built around five priorities:

1. Family and marriage.
2. Health and recovery.
3. AI school and skill growth.
4. Customer trust and pilot execution.
5. Product and company growth.

The order matters. If the founder breaks down, the company slows down.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

### Monday: Company Control Day

Primary focus:

- Review product status.
- Review data from the prior week.
- Check GitHub / backups.
- Review Cloudflare deployment status if needed.
- Identify one product improvement.
- Review pilot readiness.

Founder output:

- One weekly Prometheus priority.
- One technical or product task.
- One customer / pilot task.
- One founder training question.

### Tuesday: Customer And Pilot Day

Primary focus:

- Pilot users.
- Customer conversations.
- Follow-ups.
- Support issues.
- Feedback collection.

Founder output:

- Check in with active users.
- Record feedback.
- Identify adoption friction.
- Update pilot notes.
- Keep communication professional and simple.

### Wednesday: AI School And Skill Growth Day

Primary focus:

- AI coursework.
- Building technical skill.
- Learning systems that improve Prometheus.

Founder output:

- Complete required school progress.
- Capture one lesson that can improve Prometheus.
- Avoid major product changes unless urgent.

### Thursday: Sales And Partnership Day

Primary focus:

- Business outreach.
- Investor practice.
- Founder story.
- Pilot pipeline.

Founder output:

- Send or draft limited high-quality outreach.
- Practice 2-3 founder questions.
- Review competitor positioning.
- Improve one business document.

### Friday: Review And Reset Day

Primary focus:

- Weekly scorecard.
- Founder reflection.
- Customer notes.
- Personal reset.

Founder output:

- What moved Prometheus forward this week?
- What drained energy?
- What should be cut?
- What must happen next week?
- What can wait?

### Saturday: Deep Work Or Field Work Day

Primary focus:

- If working field sales: collect real-world data.
- If not working field sales: deep product or website work.

Founder output:

- One meaningful build session or one meaningful field-data session.
- Export backup if data was entered.
- Avoid starting five new ideas at once.

### Sunday: Strategy And Recovery Day

Primary focus:

- Light planning.
- Family time.
- Recovery.
- Founder clarity.

Founder output:

- Prepare Monday plan.
- Review upcoming week.
- Do not overload the day.
- Stop early if the essential plan is complete.

## Daily Minimum Prometheus Standard

On busy days, the founder only needs to complete the minimum standard:

- Record any real field activity or pilot data.
- Write one note about what Prometheus learned.
- Check for urgent customer or pilot messages.
- Do one small action that moves the company forward.

If those are done, the day counts.

## Daily Maximum Rule

Do not let Prometheus consume the entire day by accident.

Daily stopping rule:

- Stop when the planned priority is complete.
- Stop when quality drops.
- Stop when family time is being damaged.
- Stop when exhaustion starts creating bad decisions.

The founder should not confuse motion with progress.

## Weekly Scorecard

Track these every week:

- Field visits logged.
- GPS verified visits.
- Leads generated.
- Units / outcomes.
- Pilot user feedback.
- Product improvements completed.
- Business outreach completed.
- Founder pitch practice completed.
- AI school progress completed.
- Sleep / health consistency.
- Marriage / family time protected.

The goal is not perfection. The goal is consistency.

## Burnout Prevention Rules

Prometheus is a long game.

Rules:

- No major business decisions while exhausted.
- No emotional pricing decisions.
- No late-night legal or investor commitments.
- No sending sensitive documents when tired.
- No scope creep during pilot preparation.
- No product rebuilds during customer testing unless necessary.
- No skipping recovery for more than two days in a row.

If energy is low:

- Do admin work.
- Review notes.
- Clean folders.
- Practice one answer.
- Sleep.

## Founder Communication Rules

With customers:

- Be clear.
- Be calm.
- Be professional.
- Do not overpromise.
- Do not reveal proprietary details.
- Ask for permission before expanding access.

With investors:

- Lead with the problem.
- Show proof.
- Be honest about stage.
- Know what is still unproven.
- Avoid hype without evidence.

With family:

- Explain the weekly plan.
- Protect time.
- Do not make every conversation about Prometheus.
- Let the people closest to you see progress without feeling replaced by the company.

## Customer Support Rhythm

If pilot users are active:

- Check messages once in the morning.
- Check messages once in the evening.
- Respond to urgent blockers quickly.
- Save non-urgent ideas for the weekly review.
- Do not rebuild features based on one emotional comment.

Support priority order:

1. App access issue.
2. Data loss or backup issue.
3. GPS verification issue.
4. Confusing workflow.
5. Feature request.
6. Cosmetic request.

## Product Decision Filter

Before building anything, ask:

- Does this help the pilot succeed?
- Does this reduce user effort?
- Does this improve trust or safety?
- Does this improve measurable outcomes?
- Does this protect the business?
- Can it wait until after the pilot?

If the answer is mostly no, do not build it yet.

## Founder Training Rhythm

Practice founder questions at least 3 nights per week.

Focus areas:

- What does Prometheus IQ do?
- Why now?
- Why you?
- Why gyms first?
- What proves it works?
- Why will customers pay?
- What makes it defensible?

Training rule:

- Shorter and clearer beats longer and emotional.
- Evidence beats passion.
- Passion matters most after the evidence is understood.

## AI School Integration

AI school is not separate from Prometheus. It is founder skill development.

Each week, capture:

- One AI concept learned.
- One way it may apply to Prometheus.
- One technical gap to close.
- One thing not to build yet.

Avoid using school as an excuse to rebuild the company every week.

## Marriage And Health Guardrails

Minimum weekly guardrails:

- Protected family time.
- Real sleep.
- Movement or exercise.
- One block with no Prometheus talk.
- One honest check-in about stress.

The company should make life larger, not smaller.

## Weekly Founder Review

Every Sunday or Monday, answer:

1. What was the biggest win?
2. What created the most stress?
3. What did customers or users teach us?
4. What did the data prove?
5. What assumption got weaker?
6. What must be done next?
7. What should be delayed?
8. What should be deleted?

## The Prometheus Operating Rule

Every week should produce one of these:

- More proof.
- More trust.
- More clarity.
- More customer learning.
- More product stability.
- More founder strength.

If a task does not create one of those, it is probably noise.
