# Fitness Connection Pitch Plan

## Goal

Use Prometheus personally, then with one coworker, then build enough proof over three months to present Prometheus to the Fitness Connection owner and COO as a monthly subscription product for outside membership sales.

Primary Fitness Connection leadership targets:

- Phil, President
- Larry, COO

Preferred strategic investor target:

- Larry, if appropriate and if there is no conflict with his role at Fitness Connection

The pitch should not be:

> I built an app. Do you want to buy it?

The pitch should be:

> We used Prometheus in the field, logged real outside sales activity, found which locations produced units, reduced random driving, and built a repeatable system that can help Fitness Connection sales teams create more memberships outside the club.

## Timeline

### Phase 1: Personal Proof

Length: now through the next 2 weeks.

Goal:

- Use Prometheus every workday.
- Log every outside stop.
- Refine the app around real field behavior.
- Build your first personal result story.

Track:

- Places visited.
- Conversations.
- Leads.
- Same-day units.
- Later units.
- Best categories.
- Best time windows.
- Locations that wasted time.
- Locations worth revisiting.
- Gas/time notes.

Proof to create:

- Before Prometheus: outside sales was based on guessing and memory.
- After Prometheus: route choices are based on logged production data.

### Phase 2: Coworker Beta

Length: starts in 2 weeks, runs 2-4 weeks.

Goal:

- Give one coworker a clean Prometheus beta.
- Let him enter his own results.
- See whether the app helps another salesperson without you controlling everything.

Rules:

- He gets his own clean data.
- He uses it daily.
- He exports results weekly.
- He gives feedback on what is easy, confusing, or missing.

Proof to create:

- Prometheus works for more than one salesperson.
- Different reps may have different strengths.
- The app can compare route production across two people.
- The concept is not only useful to Cruz.

### Phase 3: Three-Month Results Window

Length: 3 months after coworker testing begins.

Goal:

- Build a serious result record before pitching leadership.
- Show real field activity, not just screenshots.
- Turn Prometheus into a repeatable outside-sales operating system.

Track weekly:

- Total outside visits.
- Total conversations.
- Total leads.
- Total same-day units.
- Total later units.
- Units per visit.
- Leads per visit.
- Best producing locations.
- Best producing categories.
- Best time windows.
- Places removed because they wasted time.
- Places revisited because they worked.
- Estimated gas/time saved by planned routes.

Best proof metrics:

- Unit increase.
- More consistent outside activity.
- Less random driving.
- Higher units per visit.
- Better location selection.
- Faster discovery of good locations.
- Repeatable route patterns.

## What To Show The Owner And COO

Do not lead with features. Lead with business outcomes.

Show:

- The problem: outside sales reps waste time guessing where to go.
- The cost: gas, time, inconsistent production, forgotten location history.
- The solution: Prometheus turns field activity into route intelligence.
- The proof: real Fitness Connection field data from you and your coworker.
- The opportunity: roll this out across clubs as a sales productivity tool.
- The business case: Fitness Connection already uses HubSpot, but Prometheus can prove it creates a field-sales layer that HubSpot does not currently provide.
- The market signal: leaders in other industries have shown interest in the Prometheus concept, suggesting this can expand beyond gyms after Fitness Connection proof.

## Outside Industry Interest

If you have spoken with leaders in other industries and they show interest, include that as early market validation.

Frame it carefully:

> Prometheus started inside gym membership sales, but I have already spoken with leaders in other outside-sales industries who understand the same problem: reps waste time and gas guessing where to go. That tells me this can begin with Fitness Connection and later expand into other field-sales markets.

Do not overstate it as committed revenue unless someone has agreed to pay.

Use three levels:

- Interest: they liked the idea or asked to see it.
- Validation: they confirmed the pain exists in their industry.
- Demand: they asked for access, pricing, a demo, or a pilot.

The strongest version is:

> Fitness Connection can be the first proven case study before Prometheus expands into other industries.

## HubSpot Positioning

Prometheus should not be pitched as a full CRM replacement at first. HubSpot is still the company system of record.

Prometheus should be pitched as the missing outside-sales operating system that sits before HubSpot:

- HubSpot tracks leads and sales records.
- Prometheus helps reps decide where to go create those leads and units.
- HubSpot shows what happened after a lead enters the system.
- Prometheus shows which outside locations, categories, routes, and time windows created the opportunity in the first place.

If the three-month test shows Prometheus helped create more units than the normal HubSpot-driven workflow alone, the pitch becomes stronger:

> We are not asking Fitness Connection to throw away HubSpot. We are showing that Prometheus produced better outside-sales intelligence and more practical field execution for a fraction of what large CRM systems cost.

If leadership is paying close to `$75,000/month` for HubSpot or related CRM costs, Prometheus should be positioned as a much lower-cost specialized layer:

- Cheaper than enterprise CRM.
- Built specifically for outside membership sales.
- Faster to use in the field.
- Focused on routes, production, gas/time savings, and location performance.
- Designed around what sales reps actually do before a lead becomes a CRM record.

The key message:

> HubSpot is broad. Prometheus is specific. HubSpot manages records. Prometheus helps outside reps produce more units.

## Cost Argument

Do not make the pitch only about being cheaper. Make it about being more targeted.

Strong framing:

> HubSpot may be necessary for company-wide CRM, but it is expensive and not built specifically to tell outside salespeople where to go today. Prometheus is a focused tool that can be offered at a much lower monthly cost while directly improving outside-sales execution.

Possible comparison:

- HubSpot: expensive company-wide CRM.
- Prometheus: lower-cost outside-sales route and production intelligence system.

Prometheus can win because it is narrow, practical, and tied directly to units.

## Suggested Pitch Structure

### 1. Opening

> I built Prometheus because outside membership sales has a hidden problem: reps spend too much time guessing where to go. Prometheus helps reps track field activity, learn which locations produce memberships, and plan better routes from real results.

### 2. The Problem

- Outside reps often rely on memory.
- Good locations get forgotten.
- Bad locations get revisited too often.
- There is no clear record of which places produce units.
- Managers cannot easily see which outside activity is working.
- Reps waste time and gas without a data-backed route plan.

### 3. The Field Test

> I tested Prometheus myself, then gave it to another sales rep. We used it to log outside visits, conversations, leads, units, time windows, and location outcomes.

Show:

- Number of days tested.
- Number of reps.
- Number of places visited.
- Number of conversations.
- Number of leads.
- Number of units.
- Best location types.
- Best time windows.

### 4. The Results

Use actual numbers after the 3-month test.

Example format:

- Reps tested: 2
- Days logged: 60
- Places visited: 180
- Conversations: 2,400
- Leads: 430
- Units: 78
- Best category: Auto/service businesses
- Best time window: Midday
- Highest-producing location: Almeda Mall corridor
- Locations removed from route: 12
- Repeatable high-value stops found: 9

Add a HubSpot comparison section if your data supports it:

- Units from Prometheus-planned outside routes.
- Units from normal outside activity before Prometheus.
- Whether Prometheus helped beat prior production averages.
- Whether Prometheus revealed locations HubSpot did not help identify.
- Whether Prometheus reduced wasted stops or random driving.

Suggested language:

> Over this test period, our Prometheus-tracked field activity produced clearer route decisions and stronger outside-sales insight than what we were getting from HubSpot alone. HubSpot kept the records, but Prometheus showed us where the production was actually coming from.

### 5. The Product

Prometheus gives reps:

- Daily route planning.
- New location ideas.
- Visit logging.
- Location performance.
- Time-window performance.
- Category performance.
- Permission tracking.
- Units-per-visit tracking.
- Data-backed route decisions.

Prometheus gives managers:

- Visibility into outside activity.
- Which locations produce units.
- Which reps are consistent.
- Which territories are worth working.
- Where outside sales should focus next.

### 6. The Ask

Start small.

Recommended ask:

> I would like to run a 90-day pilot with a few Fitness Connection sales reps and one manager dashboard. If it helps reps create more outside units and reduce wasted route time, Fitness Connection can license Prometheus monthly per club or per sales team.

## Subscription Offer Ideas

Start with a pilot, then a subscription.

Possible pricing models:

- Per club: monthly fee for one location.
- Per rep: monthly fee per salesperson.
- Per region: monthly fee for multiple clubs.
- Company license: larger monthly or annual contract.

Starter pitch:

- Pilot: free or low-cost for 90 days.
- After pilot: monthly subscription.
- Pricing depends on number of clubs/reps.

Do not lock pricing too early. First prove value.

## Larry As Potential VC

If Larry is both COO and a potential investor, treat that carefully.

Best framing:

> Larry could be a strategic advisor or investor only if it is appropriate, transparent, and separate from any Fitness Connection purchasing decision.

Important:

- Do not make it sound like Fitness Connection must buy Prometheus for Larry to invest.
- Do not ask Larry to personally invest while he is making a company purchasing decision unless everything is transparent and handled professionally.
- If serious money or a company contract is involved, talk to a qualified attorney.
- Keep the Fitness Connection customer pitch separate from any personal investment discussion.

Clean structure:

- Meeting 1: Fitness Connection business pilot discussion with Phil and Larry.
- Meeting 2: separate investor/advisor conversation with Larry only if he expresses interest.
- Any investment discussion should be documented and handled outside the company purchasing process.

- Exported data backups.
- Before/after notes.
- Coworker feedback.
- List of top-producing locations.
- List of locations removed because they wasted time.
- Daily route examples.

## Leadership Pitch Mindset

The owner and COO will care about:

- More memberships.
- Better sales team execution.
- Less wasted payroll time.
- Better manager visibility.
- Repeatable systems.
- Whether this can scale beyond one person.

They will not care as much about:

- The code.
- The app being cool.
- The name alone.
- Every small feature.

Lead with business results.

## One-Sentence Pitch

Prometheus helps outside sales reps stop guessing where to go by turning real field activity into smarter routes, better location targeting, and higher membership production.

## Three-Month Success Target

By the time you pitch, aim to show:

- Two reps used Prometheus.
- At least 8-12 weeks of real data.
- Clear top-producing categories.
- Clear locations worth revisiting.
- Clear locations to stop visiting.
- Improvement in units per visit or route efficiency.
- A practical plan to pilot it at more Fitness Connection clubs.
